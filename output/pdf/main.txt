    The reference and proposed predictor share a Neo-
Hookean constitutive family, while the native physical base-
lines retain their original constitutive representations. The
comparison therefore measures these adapted predictors
within the declared family. The matched material-indexed-
load ablation isolates the coordinate-frame choice using the
same constitutive model and inverse solver. Known geome-
try, synthetic correspondence, and settled conservative me-
chanics make this a controlled study of physical prediction;
dynamic impact and release, real visual correspondence,
and broader constitutive families are natural extensions.
