What do the images and simulation actually support?

The mechanics formulas and discrete balances pass, but the historical integration step is too coarse for millimetre-level physical claims. Its position error against a converged independent RK4 solution is about 3.3 mm (up to 11.5 mm at a node/time). More views address a different limitation. The renderer still has depth-order discontinuities.

Actual images and reconstruction

Native images are 24×24 pixels from a coloured 30-Gaussian volumetric proxy. Tiles are enlarged by nearest-neighbor display; no detail was invented. Input noise is clipped only for display, not inference. All geometry, appearance, density and cameras are known in these tests.

Independent simulation audit

Force agrees with an independently differentiated scalar strain energy to 6.7e-16 N. Free-node momentum, internal impulse torque and total angular balance including support reactions agree at floating-point precision. Supports stay fixed and all audited determinants remain positive. The RK4 reference uses the same FEM force law but an independent fourth-order integration routine.

Audit conclusion

There are three separate issues: sparse observations and known-scene assumptions; insufficient time-integration accuracy in the historical toy setup; and nonsmooth visibility in the renderer. Increasing image count cannot validate the simulator or repair its numerical visibility objective. Preserve historical results as discretized-model experiments, use converged reference data, then address integration/visibility before scientific physical-identification or calibration claims.
