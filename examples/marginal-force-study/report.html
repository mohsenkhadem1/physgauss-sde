Eliminating unknown forces analytically

Same irregular monocular observations, force prior, geometry, mechanics and observation-only warm start for alternating, joint and marginalized trajectory solvers.

Exact force elimination; approximate trajectory uncertainty. 3/12 fits pass the local stationarity screen; 5/12 pass the independent importance-weight concentration screen. Neither screen establishes calibrated uncertainty, unique mechanics/force recovery or global convergence. Shaded force bands are provisional local-Gaussian/conditional estimates.

What is compared

The new model adds fixed full-rank independent free-node velocity diffusion of 0.02 (m/s)/√s. This makes a proper trajectory density. All three new solvers use exactly this model. It differs from the historical model's absent or balanced low-rank neural internal noise. Historical rows use identical input images but cannot isolate a marginalization effect.

Alternating solves the Gaussian force conditional then updates mechanics/trajectory with force fixed. Joint optimizes force and trajectory together. Marginalized integrates all 15 force coefficients and retains their conditional covariance. All optimize the same state/velocity coordinate density; fixed covariance makes joint-profile and marginalized trajectory objectives differ by a constant.
