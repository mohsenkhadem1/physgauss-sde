Independent final pose/contact baseline audit

Decision: acceptable to retain as a frozen, disclosed approximate pose baseline.
No evidence of extra observation or future object target access in predictor or
campaign. The pose fit uses exactly parent prefix_frames and point_ids, with a
shared-validity mask. Future controller and activation are prescribed inputs.
Tests independently perturb unselected prefix frames and points with no output
change, reject future observation arrays, and reject evaluator fields. The campaign
verifies the source prediction seal and input manifest/digest before deriving output.

Held-shape preservation: pose layer applies a proper rigid transform to each raw
FEM node shape. Plane correction adds one vertical translation to the whole cloud.
Therefore held pairwise node distances are preserved. At prescribed release, shape
is frozen and the archived unit-mass rigid-cloud integrator continues gravity,
friction-capped impulses and vertical penetration projection. This does NOT model
elastic contact deformation, reaction identification, momentum exchange with the
hand, calibrated restitution, or inferred slip. Policy constants are explicit.

The supplied floor is initial min-z, an approximate known input plane rather than
measured future contact geometry. Enforcing its nonpenetration by projection is
not an independent physical sanity success. No coupled FEM/energy/physical-accuracy
claim is supported. Sample shape variability is retained but pose is point-estimated;
intervals do not capture full pose/contact uncertainty. Original forecast failures
remain in completion metadata; no new prediction-based sample filtering occurs.
