Campaign scientific review — Gaussian boundary flow v02
Snapshot: 2026-11-04. This review adds no experiments and changes no prior results.

Decision: retain the four-component diagonal mixture as the controlled predictive candidate, retain flow as a joint-uncertainty comparator, and prioritize real forward-model adequacy and prospective benchmark evaluation. The campaign demonstrates useful conditional prediction and diagnostic progress. It has not demonstrated a new external-dataset aggregate win, calibrated real physical inference, or superior active probing.

1. Controlled prediction: supported, with a narrow scope.
The corrected experiment uses 8,192 identical clean training worlds, exactly one 50 micrometre observation-noise draw, matched training/parameter budgets and 32 forward samples for each probabilistic method. On 128 new worlds (seed 10035), future coordinate RMSE in mm for sine/release/triangle/reverse is flow 0.189/0.176/0.185/0.193, diagonal mixture 0.151/0.112/0.143/0.176, and point regression 0.310/0.288/0.306/0.299. Paired mixture-minus-flow intervals exclude zero for the first three programs, not reverse. The mixture has better predictive interval scores in all four. This is known-geometry, six-parameter, same-prior block inference with command-waveform changes; it is not general boundary discovery or an external benchmark win. Mixture support-location 90% coverage is only 76.6%, so good motion predictions do not imply trustworthy parameter intervals. [matched-results.json; matched-review.txt]

A separately frozen 128-world confirmation (seed 13401) did not justify switching to the two-component full-covariance mixture. Support-location coverage increases 78.9% to 85.2%, but the paired change interval includes zero; intervals widen and parameter error/interval score worsen. Its mean forecast errors and interval scores are worse than the diagonal mixture in all four programs, although paired forecast-error intervals include zero. Flow has the best normalized joint parameter energy score (0.0538 versus diagonal 0.0863 and full covariance 0.0994). There is no single winner for all uncertainty objectives. Component count and one training seed limit attribution solely to covariance. [full-covariance-results.json; full-covariance-review.txt]

2. Mechanics/identifiability: useful bounded evidence.
A four-world two-parameter reference posterior shows strong stiffness–support-location correlations (absolute correlation 0.79–0.98). All four reference solutions ultimately meet unchanged integration gates, but one initially failed; a separately frozen fixed-spacing refinement resolves it. The other four physical parameters are fixed to truth. Four covered truths are not a calibration study, and these conditional intervals must not be compared to six-parameter unconditional model intervals. [reference-posterior/summary.json; reference-posterior/world2-fixed-spacing.json]

A 6 mm spatially varying command produces about 90% nonrigid added motion, passes the declared development strain/Jacobian gates, and improves flow versus the old neural comparator on an exposed 128-world regression cohort: coordinate error 0.191 versus 0.342 mm; aligned-shape error 0.036 versus 0.062 mm. This fixes the nearly rigid command example, but is not fresh external OOD evidence or a matched-mixture result. [differential-action-centered-z-6mm/{development,comparison}.json]

3. Real-data prefix improvement is not forecast improvement.
On historically exposed single_lift_sloth, wider boundary hypotheses and geometry corrections reduce raw prefix fit from 9.17 to 5.72 mm. The latest prefix-only classical oracle remains evaluation-budget limited. Its raw future coordinate error is 32.28 mm, versus earlier flow 31.87, prefix persistence 38.48 and velocity 35.46; its tracking correction gives 33.13 mm. Aligned-shape and manual-distance metrics improve slightly/more respectively, but primary forecast error does not improve. These are one sequence, not independent frames or a matched SOTA comparison. The original amortized bank has severe context mismatch (real standardized norm 33.8 versus bank maximum 9.88; residual outside the feature subspace 7.04 mm). The oracle's absent bank warning is not evidence that this bank became adequate. Real posterior calibration remains unestablished. [oracle-single_lift_sloth.json; geometry-oracle.json; sloth-object-scores.json; sloth-geometry-oracle-scores.json]

Initial geometry filtering removes substantial convex-hull volume, but one 64-node zebra mesh becomes disconnected. Retain connectedness/mass gates; do not apply filtering blindly. Initial video evidence supports a table hypothesis. New conservative preload and prestrain operators have derivative/objectivity tests, not demonstrated benchmark gains. [initial-geometry-audit.json; boundary-preload-review.txt; object-prestrain-review.txt]

4. Dynamics: separate qualified release from unqualified impact.
The isolated safeguarded solver qualifies weak and strong finite-range cohesive attachments over three timesteps for two 0.15 s release worlds: finest admitted drops about 85.1 and 0.55 mm, residual <=1e-7, trajectory refinement <=0.1 mm and energy error <=1% of common free-fall energy. This is mainly rigid falling, without floor impact; the energy normalization is permissive for the strong-grasp case. Finer runs still fail and remain recorded. It is a local cohesive-bond example, not learned Coulomb contact or a qualified broad dynamics bank. Impact/rebound remains unqualified: time refinement changes rebound materially and finer midpoint runs fail unchanged solver gates. Do not use these impacts as ground-truth evidence of physical realism. [release-solver-audit.txt; object-dynamics-audit.txt]

5. Available data and claims.
SoftVTBench: 600 local simulation episodes, only three recurring assets; 220 historically exposed. Local HDF5s have no RGB. The proposal reserves 60 validation, 60 calibration and 180 confirmation episodes, but is not a frozen seal and fresh arrays remain unread. PhysTwin: 22 local real RGB sequences (7,269 images), all historically exposed; materials/forces lack trusted truth and novel unrecorded views lack image ground truth. PokeFlex has code but no local raw release; license access is outstanding. Deform360 is paused by user. Existing Isaac episodes/contact regressions remain preserved; historical tests are exposed. No new rendering-quality gain is established here. [dataset-audit.txt; softvt-protocol-proposal.json]

Next two concrete benchmark experiments
A. PhysTwin forward-adequacy/forecast ablation on the four already exposed plush readiness cases (single_lift_sloth, single_push_sloth, single_lift_zebra, double_lift_zebra). Freeze identical prefix observations, masks, geometry budget and optimizer budget. Compare nested geometry/table/preload hypotheses, existing amortized models, persistence and velocity; include native PhysTwin only with a documented executable matched-input recipe. Keep raw mechanical states and tracking maps separate. Report all four cases, failed solves, coordinate/rigid-aligned shape error and available held-recorded-view image metrics. Never fit on suffix frames. Admit a new bank only after prefix support is adequate; forecast improvement is an independent requirement. This is exposed-case diagnosis, not a new sealed test.
B. Freeze the proposed SoftVT 60/60/180 validation/calibration/confirmation split across all six suite/task cells. Develop on the exposed 220, explicitly report the more deforming pastry002 task, then freeze sources/checkpoints before confirmation reads. Compare frozen diagonal mixture, flow, point regressor, persistence/velocity and a runnable established baseline under the same privileged geometry/position-only observation budget. Primary metric: mean per-episode future coordinate RMSE, paired episode uncertainty and all failures; add aligned shape, pose and joint predictive interval score/coverage from stored full sample trajectories. Use calibration only for a declared predictive calibration procedure. This evaluates new episodes of recurring assets, not unseen-object OOD, RGB inference or official manipulation success.
