Read-only decision audit: archived slip versus unilateral pushing

Recommendation: finish the frozen eight-case comparison, then run ONE matched prefix-only bilateral-versus-unilateral forward-adequacy oracle on single_push_sloth. Do not restore gravity-slip on PhysTwin merely because prefix velocity currently beats the pose/contact candidates. No new candidate or evaluation was run for this audit.

Why archived slip is not a drop-in PhysTwin fix
- softvt_slip.py explicitly restricts itself to SoftVTBench. Its hypothesis is object slip downward relative to an active grasp: estimate gravity-aligned velocity in the controller frame, integrate lost overlap, and force release after half an initial object extent. It needs grasp and gripper_command semantics. PhysTwin supplies neither a verified held-grasp state nor that actuator command; its attachment_active array is constructed as ones. A pushing hand moving away is not equivalent to a soft object slipping down between two fingers.
- The archived estimator uses the last EIGHT CONSECUTIVE observations after grasp, whereas the current frozen methods see TWELVE SPACED prefix frames and 48 selected points. Calling the old function on all available prefix tracks would silently increase the observation budget. Reusing its formula on the last eight selected spaced frames changes the temporal fit/window and must be labelled a different, newly frozen candidate, not restoration of the same validated recipe.
- Velocity outperforming controller-transport predictions is evidence of a useful empirical motion baseline. It does not identify gravity slip, release timing or contact topology. The constant controller offset assumption can fail through nonadhesive pushing, nonrigid deformation, rotation, tracking bias or force mismatch. Current shape errors and discarded-PCA mismatch remain substantial; a centroid-only slip law cannot explain them.
- A later SoftVT-only slip restoration is scientifically reasonable as an explicitly limited empirical baseline when held-grasp metadata is available, shared-prefix observations are enforced and archived overlap/release semantics are preserved. It should not be tuned on the new suffix scores or advertised as contact inference. It is not the next physics-identification experiment.

Why the unilateral oracle is better targeted
The source contains a specific falsifiable structural hypothesis: permanent bilateral springs exert tensile force after tool separation. The isolated unilateral operator has verified conservative derivatives and a qualified 64-node push/withdraw example with zero force after separation. The real pushing prefix supplies a highly linear inward command axis (99.907% first principal component), although this remains an assumed plane normal rather than a measured surface normal. Three declared standoffs at median prior parameters solve but fit poorly (relative prefix RMSE34.68–35.52mm). That leaves forward-family adequacy versus parameter estimation unresolved; more density capacity cannot answer it.

The single proposed experiment
Use the corrected single_push_sloth predictor package, identical filtered geometry/table/uniform material, 12 selected prefix frames, 48 selected points, response-ratio bounds and solver gates. Fit the same five mechanical response parameters for each boundary law using a matched total number of forward evaluations and a fixed wall-time cap, all declared BEFORE starting. For the unilateral law retain only the already declared {-10,0,+10}mm standoffs; share the total budget across these three hypotheses. Give the bilateral family the same total budget with three predetermined starts. Do not quietly add a wider offset/normal search if the trial fails.

Primary diagnostic: raw relative-prefix coordinate RMSE and rigid-aligned prefix shape error, reported alongside absolute-prefix error, maximum solver residual, minimum J, completed/failed calls, and the frozen plane assumption. Use no posterior training and no suffix evaluation in this first experiment. Preserve every hypothesis/start, not just its minimum. A lower prefix error shows better observed-response adequacy, not future accuracy or identified true contact. If the unilateral family cannot materially cover the prefix, report that and reconsider plane geometry/material/prestress; do not train a new amortized bank over the same inadequate family. Any subsequent forecast comparison must be a separately frozen experiment after this diagnostic.
