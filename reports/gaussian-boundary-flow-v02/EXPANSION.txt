Protocol
Eight historically exposed development cases, not a new sealed generalization test.
128 accepted simulator worlds per case;5,000 training steps;32 inference draws;
12 selected prefix frames;48 tracked points. Raw and derived methods use identical
within-case observations, commands, meshes and evaluation identities. Every source,
selection and output was sealed before its evaluator process opened suffix targets.
Seven cases use64nodes; initial-geometry qualification required120 for double-zebra.
Corrected affine transport and correlated relative-coordinate noise are shared.
Mechanics/retrieval receive future tool commands; velocity uses only the prefix.
The rigid contact layer uses supplied floor and approximate impulses/projection;
zero penetration after projection is not evidence of recovered contact forces.

Independent audit verified identity/clock/geometry/point-mask consistency and all
source/output hashes. A provenance-check-only code amendment is recorded explicitly;
it did not change retrieval selection or mechanics. Every failure remains available.
