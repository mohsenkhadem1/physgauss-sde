MATCHED CLEAN-NOISE INFERENCE COMPARISON — completed 4 November 2026

Frozen protocol
8192 clean 64-node-block simulations, seed 43091. One 50 micrometre acquisition
noise draw per minibatch, shared minibatch/noise streams. Each model trained for
15000 AdamW steps with batch256. All checkpoints were frozen before constructing
the new 128-case test cohort (seed10035). All methods receive the exact same noisy
test context. Flow and four-component diagonal Gaussian mixture each propagate
32 physical samples; the deterministic model propagates one estimate.
Capacities: flow141894, mixture141713, deterministic140996 parameters.

Mean future coordinate RMSE (mm)
Program       Flow       Mixture    Deterministic
Sine          0.1885     0.1508      0.3097
Release       0.1765     0.1118      0.2881
Triangle      0.1854     0.1433      0.3064
Reversal      0.1934     0.1759      0.2990

The mixture improves the mean over flow in all four programs. Paired, case-level
bootstrap 95% intervals exclude zero for sine, release and triangle; reversal is
inconclusive (mixture-minus-flow interval [-0.0513,+0.0134] mm).
Flow beats the deterministic logit-MSE baseline with intervals excluding zero
for all four. The deterministic model is not yet optimized with trajectory loss.

Uncertainty
Mixture coordinate coverage is 91.4-92.4% for nominal 90% intervals; flow coverage
is 95.7-96.3%. Mixture interval scores (lower is better) are also lower for all
four programs: 0.393-0.649 mm versus flow0.784-0.844 mm. Both use 32-sample
empirical quantiles, so these are finite-sample estimates, not exact population
quantiles or a formal 90% guarantee. These are pointwise coordinate intervals;
whole-trajectory coverage is separately recorded and is much lower.

Physical-parameter caution
Better predictive intervals do NOT establish well-calibrated boundary inference.
Mixture support-x coverage is only76.6%, versus97.7% for flow; mixture load-position
coverage is82.8%. Flow also has82.8% load-position coverage. Thus neither method
should be sold as a trustworthy physical-parameter posterior without reference
posterior/SBC checks and better observation-model validation.

Decision recommendation
Use the mixture as the preferred economical predictive-inference candidate for
external development adapters, retaining flow as a comparator. It samples directly
without a generative ODE. Let held-out-object development metrics decide, rather
than requiring flow matching because it is newer. Preserve the result as a
controlled development comparison: all objects share one geometry, known mass,
known initial state, supplied top/bottom boundary topology, Gaussian widths,
force direction and future command. OOD here means changed future command only.
