INITIAL NATURAL-STRAIN EXTENSION: DECISION REVIEW AND VALIDATION
2026-11-04; /root/flow_numerical_audit. No fits or GPU runs performed.

Decision
The proposed multiplicative natural-metric model is mechanically consistent and
useful as a small nested hypothesis for initial loaded-shape mismatch. It is not
a complete explanation for that mismatch and should not be added to every bank
without a forward qualification. Large initial rigid sag can come from weak
attachments relative to gravity; internal prestrain cannot create a missing net
support reaction. Keep s=0 as a baseline and compare only after root review.

Implementation
New, isolated file: scripts/gaussian_boundary_flow_v02/object_prestrain.py
Class: InitialPrestrainFEM(ExternalFEM)
Existing object backends/campaigns and banks are untouched.

Let a be a supplied material-frame unit axis (default z), and h_e in [0,1] the
initial element-center height along that axis. One appended unconstrained latent
q gives s=0.3*tanh(q), and
  A_e = I + (exp(s h_e)-1) a a^T,
  W_ref(F,A) = det(A)^(-1) W_NH(F A).
Stress-free elastic deformation satisfies F A=I. A is fixed in material
coordinates; it is not recomputed from the deformed shape. Spatially varying
natural metrics need not integrate to a single globally compatible rest mesh,
so this represents an eigenstrain/residual-strain hypothesis, not an identified
natural geometry. The current single mode is deliberately restrictive.

The det(A)^(-1) factor converts energy per natural volume to energy per chosen
observed-reference volume. Existing lumped masses remain fixed. This convention
corresponds to natural density rho_natural=rho_reference*det(A) if interpreting
both configurations volumetrically; it does not silently change total mass.
The gravity/material ratio remains a response ratio, not absolute density or E.

Analytic derivatives
Let Fe=F A, Pe=dW_NH/dFe, and Ce=dPe/dFe. Then
  P = det(A)^(-1) Pe A^T,
  C_iJkL = det(A)^(-1) Ce_iMkN A_JM A_LN.
The new helper returns W, P and C analytically. The subclass replaces only the
bulk contribution by subtracting the original bulk and adding this response.
All gravity, attachment, floor, optional field/compliance and inertial terms are
preserved. The existing solver and its line search are reused; no body load is
introduced to cancel residuals. One extra latent is appended after the original
parameter vector; i_prestrain/parameter_count expose the layout.

What is guaranteed/tested
- Objectivity: W(RF,A)=W(F,A) for proper spatial rotations; P transforms as R P.
- Bulk internal forces have zero net force and zero current-configuration torque.
- s=0 reduces to the existing energy/residual/Hessian to numerical precision.
- Analytic Piola/tangent agree with autograd and central finite differences.
- Assembled FEM residual/Hessian agree with autograd; prestrain derivatives pass.
- Existing mass and gravitational resultant remain unchanged after support removal.
- Initial natural strain cannot balance net gravity when compliant anchors are
  unextended and a penalty floor has zero penetration. That limitation is tested.
The inherited weak positional gauge is unchanged; the module does not claim an
ideal freefall simulator. It verifies that prestrain adds no external resultant.

Important initial-state limitation
At the observed reference x=X0, all current attachment targets also equal X0 and
floor penetration is zero. Thus attachment and penalty-floor reactions are zero.
The sum of any objective internal prestrain force is zero, leaving nonzero net
gravity. Exact equilibrium at precisely that shape is therefore impossible unless
there is a valid support reaction/preload, a hard constraint capable of reaction
at zero displacement, or finite compliance displacement. A localized attachment
preload is a boundary hypothesis; a persistent distributed residual-cancelling
body force is not an acceptable substitute. This extension does not add either.
