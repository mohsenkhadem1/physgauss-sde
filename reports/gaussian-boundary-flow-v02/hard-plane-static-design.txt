Isolated static hard-plane contact candidate — CPU qualification

Files: hard_plane_static.py and hard_plane_qualification.py in the v02 script
package. Original ExternalFEM and all frozen benchmark/validation dependencies are
unchanged. No benchmark, training, impact or dynamic rollout was performed.

Mechanics
Minimize the existing bulk + gravity + attachment (+ optional external field)
energy subject to every mechanical node satisfying z>=known floor. The subclass
removes both the old soft normal penalty and tiny positional gauge from objective,
residual and Hessian. It rejects the separate tangential penalty and inertial terms.
For a lower bound, stationarity is grad(V)-lambda*ez=0, lambda>=0, gap>=0,
lambda*gap=0. The returned normal reactions are these KKT multipliers, not an
estimated force channel or a posthoc translation that merely hides penetration.
Units of reported reactions are the FEM's dimensionless energy-gradient units;
absolute Newton forces still require the material/length scaling convention.

Solver
An active-set reduced Newton direction holds bound DOFs with compressive gradients.
Contact DOFs needing tensile reaction are released into the free set. Newton damping
is numerical optimization only; it adds no energy term. A feasible tangent-cone
fallback handles unsuitable directions, and fraction-to-bound line search prevents
penetration. Bound rounding within machine precision occurs inside optimization,
not on exported predictions. Armijo energy descent has a safeguarded near-convergence
roundoff case requiring residual reduction, as in the separately reviewed Newton
candidate. No negative-J state is accepted by the bulk evaluator. Penetrating initial
states are rejected rather than mechanically reinterpreted by projection.

Acceptance checks
Stationarity norm<=1e-8; penetration<=1e-12 nondimensional; complementarity<=1e-10;
positive determinantJ>.03. Reactions are nonnegative and zero on separated nodes.
Report original residual minus contact reaction, total force and torque balances.
No friction/restitution/slip dynamics are claimed. The frictionless plane leaves
horizontal translation/yaw underdetermined if no attachments fix them; removing
that ambiguity must use an explicit gauge/observation convention, not fake springs.

Five tests pass: supported gravity and reaction balance; increased reaction under
push; lift/release without tension; energy/residual/tangent autograd consistency
and exact invariance to the ignored legacy soft-floor slot; rejection of invalid
initial penetration and dynamics. Qualification JSON preserves every attempted case;
there were no failed physical trials. An initial test-code Tensor-minus-list typo
was fixed without changing solver/physics.

Before any inference or benchmark adoption
The inherited theta floor coordinate is ignored ONLY to reuse ExternalFEM indexing.
A future backend MUST remove it from latent names/bounds/count; no unobservable
soft-floor stiffness may be trained or reported. A new model contract/bank is needed.
Known floor height is a premise, not inferred contact truth. Connected mesh and
finite-J/force/KKT admission must be checked on each actual geometry and parameter
world. Hard constraints can expose incompatibility with prescribed attachments;
keep failures. Node-only nonpenetration on linear tetrahedra defines this plane
geometry, but does not supply curved geometry, object-object contact or friction.
Current fresh validation remains under its frozen old recipe and is untouched.
