DECISION
The proposed bridge is a coherent restricted physical-code inference experiment, conditional on the implementation gates below. It is not evidence for the six-parameter FEM, spatial contact discovery, calibrated mechanics or novel-force-family OOD. No trained accuracy exists at audit time. The strict cache/campaign is still under construction and needs its own review before execution.

CONFIRMED CONTRACT
Five prefix rows x119 features include camera/time and explicit scale normalization. Encoder context128 plus4 boundary and4 force one-hots gives136; ConditionalFlow predicts25 unconstrained coordinates. PhysicalParameterTransform performs sigmoid plus bounded affine physical mapping, not teacher normalization. Teacher subsequently applies its own physical affine normalization. The code contains logs of E/rho/damping, nu, four spatial centers, two force vectors and support motion amplitude. Inactive support/force/motion canonicalization is a many-to-one decoding operation, not a probability-density Jacobian. The second load center remains a teacher feature even when inactive, so nuisance correlations are possible.

TRAINING/SAMPLING
The existing flow objective is valid linear Gaussian-to-label conditional flow matching, MSE against target-minus-noise, with Heun sampling. Its integration clock is artificial generative time. It is not a mechanics ODE or likelihood; comparisons to archived NLL are invalid. True category conditioning during training and independently sampled categorical heads during inference are an explicit factorization assumption. Standard Gaussian flow base and bounded transform do not constitute an externally specified physical prior; the empirical training distribution supplies learned prior information. Four known forcing families do not demonstrate unseen force-type generalization.

GATES REPORTED TO IMPLEMENTER
1. Existing predict_particles accepts one-case physical[S,25]/categories[S], not adapter[B,S,25]. Select each case or implement correctly repeated batched geometry, and test B=2,S=3 against per-case decoding. The plan's batched helper description needs correction.
2. Validate positive integer integration steps. Frozen ConditionalFlow.sample with steps=0 silently returns the base distribution. Wrapper must reject this, nonfinite/invalid category weights and invalid sampled outputs. Source implementation remains frozen.
3. If logit standardization is used, fit and persist TRAIN-only mean and strictly positive scales with a declared variance floor; both flow and MDN require exactly the same forward/inverse affine map. Undo this before sigmoid physical decoding. Fixed padded/inactive dimensions need explicit treatment. Training-label tolerance/clipping is supervised dequantization, not observed uncertainty; count out-of-support labels before clipping and report transform saturation.
4. Role-check and whitelist paths before private supervision access. Generic old loaders open future images or inspect every recipe before filtering. Strict cache must not inherit that behavior. Hash training-derived transforms and teacher/source/checkpoint bindings.
5. Shared frozen teacher must decode effective physical values and receive full reference geometry consistently. Training-only smoke must check dimensions/finite outputs; teacher agreement is not independent physical validity. Preserve oracle teacher error as evaluator-only diagnostic.

UNCERTAINTY
32 validation draws imply about1–2 samples per5% tail;128 final draws imply about6.4. Quantile intervals and material coverage will have Monte Carlo variability. They are descriptive predictive/posterior spreads until empirical coverage and proper scores demonstrate otherwise. Fixed validation seeds reduce selection noise but do not calibrate probabilities. Report active coordinates by category, failure denominators, layout-group uncertainty and repeatable sampling. Inactive padding intervals must not be interpreted as recovered physical parameters. Independent categories and restricted known force envelopes limit any posterior completeness claim.
