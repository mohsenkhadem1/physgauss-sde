Scientific scope of this Isaac replay
These predictors infer latent physical/load-family hypotheses from the prefix under their learned training prior. Unknown future forcing is NOT identified or guaranteed predictable; there is no justified claim for arbitrary new force processes. Both probabilistic inverse methods and ridge share the same learned forward operator, so this compares inverse inference under that teacher, not independent physical simulators/SOTA. The272episodes have gravity=0 and lack established numerical convergence. Later error metrics can establish regression prediction quality, not true physical-material calibration. The old historical tests are exposed. The current v03 six-parameter nodal model has no existing feature encoder or unknown-input process bridge, so labelling this replay as a v03 improvement would be false.
