POST-VALIDATION DECISION AUDIT
2026-11-05. Read only the two existing score-summary JSONs; no new predictions, raw targets, fitting, edits to methods, or runs.

DECISION
Preserve every frozen method and previous result. Do not adopt global deformation shrinkage, residual cancellation, a per-case winner selector, or a larger network. The completed evidence supports a useful motion/shape decomposition and a small flow-vs-retrieval advantage on SoftVT, not general physical-inference superiority. Any next change needs a new prospective protocol; these validation outcomes are now development evidence for that change.

WHAT THE NUMBERS SUPPORT
SoftVT: 11/12 physics cases complete (object-soft-task1-demo50 missing/failed). Flow raw49.714mm coordinateRMSE becomes19.245mm with existing pose/contact wrapper; aligned shape goes1.568→1.743mm. Most coordinate improvement is therefore transport/contact correction, not better elastic inference. On the SAME11cases, flow_pose_contact beats nearest_pose_contact by0.267mm coordinate and0.379mm shape, but coordinatewins only5/11. Against rigid_contact it is WORSE by0.191mm coordinate and0.544mm shape (wins4/11 and2/11). Persistence shape is also stronger: flow shapeerror worse by0.981mm onpairedcases, despite vastly better coordinates. This is compatible with weak observable deformation; it does not identify stiffness uniquely.

PhysTwin all8: rawflow49.242mm, rawMDN31.568mm, rawnearest30.794mm. Their shapeerrors19.817,19.929,18.932mm are relatively close. Thus inference/posterior transport differences matter materially in the raw flow result; blaming everything on the forward model would be unjustified. After the same pose/contact wrapper, flow37.242mm, MDN37.264mm, nearest37.024mm: inverse-network choice then matters little. The wrapper HELPS rawflow but HURTS MDN andnearest, so it is not universally beneficial. Flow contact beats rigidcontact39.776mm in6/8cases and by4.845mm alignedshape onaverage: deformable modelling does contain useful signal. Yet nearestcontact is0.218mm better overall, and velocity33.191mm beatsflowcontact on6/8cases. The two double-stretch cases favorflow overvelocity, while six othercases do not. Preserve that regime-level evidence without using it to construct a posthoc selector.

ERROR ATTRIBUTION
1. Rigid transport is a major bottleneck on SoftVT: raw-versus-wrapper effect dwarfs flow-versus-MDN/retrieval differences. Existing wrapper ALREADY carries rigid state/velocity afterrelease; do not propose adding that samefeature again.
2. Inference error or posterior aggregation is a plausible contributor to PhysTwin rawflow49mm versusMDN31.6mm. Scores alone cannot distinguish bad latent means, rare failed/large-displacement draws, multimodality or solver-conditioned prior. That requires a fixed diagnostic, not assertions.
3. Shared forward/support/material/initial-state mismatch remains plausible because methods share it and contact-corrected predictions converge near37mm. These metrics do not isolate its cause or prove a materiallaw failure. Similar errors also arise if observations cannot identify response.
4. Pose correction and posterior-mean trajectories are prediction devices, not proofs of force balance. A mean of valid nonlinear states need not itself be a valid equilibrium.

ADOPTION / REJECTION
Adopt the strong rigid_contact and nearest-bank methods as permanent controls and practical reference outputs. Keep flow/MDN/raw/tracking/contact separate in the paper; do not call the best outcome percase one algorithm. Do not silently replace the old best model with a variant that worsens other regimes.
Reject posthoc scaling of deformation toward the initial shape as a physics improvement. It might reduce measured shape error here, but it generally changes constitutive stress without recomputing external loads/contact, invalidating equilibrium and uncertainty. A declared statistical predictor could be tested later with TRAIN/validation-only regularization; no parameter selected from these outcomes or claim of improvedmechanics.
Reject cancelling the initial equilibrium residual with a persistent bodyforce: this can cancel gravity afterrelease and invent support. Initialprestrain or localized attachment preload are physically interpretable latent alternatives, but require reaction/uncertainty evidence and freshqualification, not automaticadoption. Rebased exports must remain labelled tracking approximations.

ONE BOUNDED NEXT DIAGNOSTIC
Before another modelchange, use existing EXPOSED development bank worlds only for a frozen same-model roundtrip: generate the unchanged causal features at known latenttheta; compare flow,MDN andnearest under the SAME forward backend, separating properrigidpose and shape errors and retaining every draw/failure. Stratify outputs by prefix feature-support diagnostic using thresholds fixedwithout forecasttargets. This directly asks whether flow itself reproduces the bank's mechanically valid predictions or introduces rawpose tails. It needs no new architecture, no newdataset, no observedfuturetarget and no tunedshrinkage. If it fails, fix conditioning/training or expose posterior-tail diagnostics before touching mechanics. If all three succeed but realprefix support remains poor, prioritize the forward/initial-state mismatch ratherthan training a largerflow.
A later coupled-elastic release extension already has a separately qualified no-impact API, but current impact regimes remainunqualified. It must not be injected into the frozenbenchmark or used to claim resolved dropping/contact accuracy.

CAMPAIGN COMPLETION IMPLICATIONS
The finite SoftVT/PhysTwin comparison is completed with explicit failure accounting, not an achieved claim that our method wins everywhere. Preserve the positive stretching/shape evidence and the small SoftVT retrieval improvement alongside stronger controls. The separate Isaac recorded-motion evaluation remains its own pending work if notfinished; this audit cannot certify rootgoal completion. Broad calibrated physicalidentification, activeprobing benefit and universally improvedOOD prediction remain research objectives unsupported by these summaries. Close completed experiments honestly; do not extend them indefinitely with architecture growth to chase a preferred result.
