Isolated unilateral Gaussian attachment implementation and mechanical checks

Implemented scripts/gaussian_boundary_flow_v02/unilateral_attachment.py. The standalone conservative operator returns energy, energy gradient and exact nodal tangent blocks for E=.5 sum(active*k*w*min(gap,0)^2). Positive gap has exactly zero force. Negative gap generates compressive normal force only; tangential displacement is free. The fixed material patch may use the existing nonnegative Gaussian weights; no state-dependent gate derivative is omitted. At gap zero, the inactive generalized tangent is chosen; the potential is C1, not C2.

UnilateralAttachmentFEM replaces only groups explicitly selected by a boolean mask. Other attachment groups retain the original bilateral spring, and an all-false mask reproduces the original FEM energy/residual/tangent exactly. Gravity, floor, bulk constitutive law, parameter count and solver are unchanged. Full prescribed plane origins and unit normals are supplied in world SI coordinates for each command frame; positions convert using the FEM's existing origin/length normalization. These are mechanical hypotheses, not inferred contact labels.

Five tests pass: potential gradient and Hessian consistency with autodifferentiation; compressive force sign; exact zero force after separation/withdrawal or activation off; tangential freedom; proper task-frame rotation/translation covariance; full FEM derivative consistency; exact nested bilateral control; invalid normal/nonfinite-input rejection. Multiple assertions are grouped within the five tests. No evaluation trajectories, new fitting or benchmark campaign were accessed or changed.
