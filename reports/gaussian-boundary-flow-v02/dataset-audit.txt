   The current six-parameter cube model cannot be applied to arbitrary geometry
   by rescaling coordinates. Add geometry/visibility-aware context and distinguish
   rigid carrier motion from deformation before calling any result a transfer.
2. SoftVTBench has 600 local episodes, not just the prior 220. Six files contain
   100 episodes each, spanning two suites, three tasks and three repeated assets.

   Suggested prospective allocation: existing220 for training/development,

   This is a proposal, not a seal. Do not inspect fresh trajectories until root

4. Isaac has272 passive episodes:48 original orbit +224 neural-boundary episodes,

Critical contract findings
- scripts/prefix_ridge_switch_v13/inputs.py::phystwin_payload uses the obsolete
  outgoing transition mask. Use incoming-v22 semantics: visibility[t] AND validity
  of transition[t-1], with visibility alone at0. Existing v19 corrected exports
  are under runs/real-transfer-v19/inputs/incoming-v22.
- The v24 Sequence.sample(t) deliberately consumes target-time RGB. It is an
  observed-video reconstruction API, NOT a causal forecasting input API.
- SoftVT local HDF5 files have no RGB arrays. Their current48 tracked-node prefix
  and initial volumetric mesh are privileged synthetic geometry. Do not describe
  that benchmark as video inference or official manipulation DSR evaluation.
- SoftVT obs/soft_extras contain force/torque and deformation diagnostics; these
  are not permitted position-only inputs. Full future tool poses/opening commands
  are allowed, but future object states are evaluator-only.
- PhysTwin materials/forces have no trustworthy truth labels. Render comparisons
  require recorded held cameras/times; unrecorded viewpoints have no RGB ground
  truth. Rope/cloth need representation qualification before solid-FEM scoring.

PokeFlex availability checked against official portal today
https://pokeflex.ait.ethz.ch/ still requires an institutional-email application
and acceptance of its noncommercial academic license. No request was submitted.
The clone at runs/third_party/PokeFlex-v19 has loaders, code and GIFs, not raw
recordings. Author paths in preprocessing/testing configs do not exist here.
Missing: approved access, raw RGB-D/robot data, processed template meshes,
sequence meshes, robot_data.json and native checkpoint files. Its native
robot-data model uses force histories; compare modalities explicitly. For our
position-only formulation, measured forces should be withheld evaluation targets.

Shared adapter recommendation
Predictor package: provenance/role/object-group/units; initial geometry; permitted
prefix observations and masks; declared optional RGB/depth/camera data; complete
known tool-position/opening schedules; explicit known-parameter flags. No true
forces/materials/contact labels, future object arrays or diagnostic summaries.
Evaluator package: future geometry/manual tracks, held RGB, valid masks and
physical labels only where actually measured. Keep it out of model constructors.
Perturb withheld arrays and verify predictor hashes unchanged. Keep object groups
intact, failures visible, pose/shape errors separate and domain-calibration disclosed.
