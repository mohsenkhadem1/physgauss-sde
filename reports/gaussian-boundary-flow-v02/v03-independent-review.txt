Independent prelaunch review: package_campaign_v03 and affine_inputs

Decision: suitable for bounded historically exposed development pilot, not a claim
of calibrated inverse physics or unseen-object generalization. No heavy jobs were
run by this reviewer.

Affine exporter reads the old immutable package, computes an initial-only affine
map and writes a new immutable package. Source arrays are not modified; export
is idempotent; attempting to overwrite the source with changed content raises.
All non-map arrays remain identical. Source manifest provenance and row norms are
persisted; new bank features must be generated for this changed observation map.

Training: bank tensors, PCA projection, context, latent targets, network parameters
and simulated observation noise reside on args.device. Thus a CUDA device argument
actually runs posterior training on that GPU. The empirical support helper moves
features to CPU deliberately; it is a small diagnostic, not training.

Resume: persisted transform is loaded rather than refitted; transform hashes bind
to bank and plan; saved models bind to transform/bank/plan. Per-model global seeds
and dedicated observation generator avoid checkpoint-skip changing the next model's
training randomness. No optimizer-state partial resume is claimed; incomplete
training restarts from its declared seed. Posterior samples and exact model/PCA
hashes seal before future-command forward simulations.

Noise: independent absolute-coordinate draws precede shared frame0 subtraction,
then mask and PCA. This correctly creates zero reference-frame noise and temporal
covariance under the declared IID absolute-error assumption. Sigma .001m remains
assumed, not measured sensor calibration. Fixed point identities are required.
Confirmed defect fixed: helper accepted NaN/infinite sigma via sigma<0-only check.
It now rejects nonfinite/negative sigma and noninteger/nonpositive dimensions before
sampling. Nine new independent validation/export tests plus checkpoint/support/
object-scorer tests passed together:37passed. No other shared implementation edits.

Support diagnostic inspects both retained and discarded PCA spaces against the
same correlated-noise bank. It explicitly labels thresholds empirical, not OOD
probabilities. An out-of-bank warning does not cause arbitrary sample rejection;
its implication must remain visible in scientific reporting.

Seals: scorer checks added schema1 inference/model/transform hashes as well as bank,
plan and prediction, before evaluation targets. Dataset audit owner confirmed
byte-tampering/incomplete-provenance/unsafe-name/evaluator-access regression tests;
adding explicit missing-file test. Checkpoint helper validates model/transform
bindings when writing seals. These are reproducibility hashes, not signatures.

Remaining declared limitations: geometry-specific feasible prior bank, approximate
quasistatic forward law, no identified initial prestress, no basket geometry for
SoftVT, no physical balance claim for tracking rebasing, finite posterior draw
count, and unvalidated physical uncertainty. These do not prevent the bounded
pilot but prevent broad calibration/contact/dynamic conclusions.
