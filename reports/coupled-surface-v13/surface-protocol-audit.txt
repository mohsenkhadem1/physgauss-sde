Decision: approve a separate, bounded extension. A development-only spatial
remeshing diagnostic identified vertex-contact aliasing of a narrow edge.
Temporal refinement does not establish spatial contact convergence. Keep the
original coupled-contact-v13 experiment, outputs and source freeze unchanged;
state its newly identified spatial limitation explicitly. This extension is
coupled-surface-v13, not a silent repair of old reserved predictions.

Surface quadrature qualification
Use rest-surface quadrature with mesh-independent target spacing<=3mm and
area weights, barycentrically transferring contact forces to the object's nodes.
Verify reference-surface area/first moments and finite nonnegative weights.
Force and torque must be conserved by the barycentric transfer: contact points
and action/reaction arms must agree. Coulomb friction and separation remain
unilateral; no force after separation. Apply identical surface contact logic to
all mechanical model classes being compared, including rigid/native adaptations.

A finer surface quadrature does not itself prove spatial convergence. On the two
development objects, compare at least two quadrature spacings and multiple body
mesh resolutions with matched physical parameters, public commands and surface
probe correspondences. The narrow edge case must be included. Check full
trajectory and contact timing/deformation, not only final positions. Report
remaining continuum/discretization error independently from inference error.
Retain absolute original reference gates (penetration<=1mm, positive volume,
base/half-dt trajectory coordinate RMS<=.25mm), and declare any added spatial
acceptance threshold before reserved generation. Do not relax these gates in
response to new reserved results. If spatial convergence is not established,
explicitly limit the resulting claim to the surface-quadrature discrete model.

The first surface-contact development pilot exceeded the fixed penetration gate
and exposed a nearly balanced edge-impact configuration. Before any fresh
reserved generation, approve a new public scene with contact stiffness 19200
instead of4800N/m at the declared reference area, and edge-drop block center
x=.018m against edge center x=.010m (8mm imbalance rather than2mm). These are
engineering changes to contact compliance and experiment geometry, not a
relaxation of numerical thresholds. Apply them to new reference, inferred FEM,
nominal/oracle FEM, rigid and native methods identically. Preserve the old pilot
and its failed stress case as separate evidence; no replacement of old outputs.

Spatial acceptance is declared before the new spatial outcomes: body meshes4
versus6cells per axis with common surface quadrature/contact law; full-trajectory

qualification matrix before generation. These are two-resolution consistency
gates, not proof of asymptotic mesh convergence or an independent physics engine.
Original timestep, penetration and positive-volume gates remain unchanged.

PRE-FREEZE ABLATION CLAIM CORRECTION
The vertex comparator retains actual nodal masses in contact damping, whereas
the surface/clipped comparator uses total body mass distributed by rest-surface
area. Even under identical scene, mesh and penalty coefficient, this changes the
damping distribution as well as contact sampling/integration. Accordingly label
it "matched scene/mesh vertex-contact formulation" and explicitly NOT a pure
quadrature-only ablation. Earlier statements that only discretization differs
are superseded. Its measured gains can support the joint contact-formulation
change, not a causal attribution solely to quadrature. No extra comparator is
required for that narrower claim; a future pure-quadrature experiment would also
match the damping-mass density. This is a clarification before new outcomes, not
a predictor revision.

PROSPECTIVE ENGINEERED SCENE AND INERTIA CANDIDATE
The current candidate uses common penalty76800 and edge-drop COM x=.030m.
The old x=.018m COM lay exactly on the support right boundary, rather than
robustly beyond it. The new COM lies12mm beyond it while the block footprint

deadline and all penetration/time/shape/body limits remain fixed.
