Numerical/statistical policy
Each stage requires both material cases for a scenario and the unchanged sparse
AND dense spatial bounds (world1mm, alignedshape.5mm), per-body center1mm,
penetration1mm, J>.02 and finite collider states. The checks retain failed stages.
Using a predeclared first-passing-pair rule on development numerical diagnostics
is appropriate engineering selection; it is not held-out forecast tuning.
However agreement of two resolutions is a consistency check, not evidence of
asymptotic continuum convergence. Passing a scene at one reference resolution
does not automatically qualify another finer reference for that scene.
