Supports material/friction fitting under known scene physics; does not establish inferred arbitrary boundary geometry, unknown support positions, unknown gravity, or general unknown force fields. Each object's parameters transfer unchanged to slide/curved/moving-sphere and exploratory edge-drop.

3. POSITION INPUT, NOT MEASURED FORCE INPUT
Supported algorithm claim: predictors receive initial geometry, observed calibration positions, time and prescribed tool position/active tracks. Contact forces are computed by the physical model rather than supplied as measured force labels. This is action-conditioned forecasting when future tool trajectories are provided, not autonomous prediction of unknown future actions. The moving sphere has dynamic state and reacts to contact; prescribed tools do not become inferred dynamic hands.
Evidence: frozen pipeline prepare_object/evaluate and inference.py; fixed protocol input schema; independent observations/selection audit. Material truth is used only for reference/oracle and explicitly post-fit diagnostics, not ordinary candidate selection.
Missing ablation: prediction from image pixels alone, noisy/incomplete tool tracking, unknown initial velocity, unobserved force sources, inferred gravity or external field.
