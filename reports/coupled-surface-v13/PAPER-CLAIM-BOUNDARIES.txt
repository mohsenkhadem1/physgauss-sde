PAPER CLAIM BOUNDARIES — evidence ledger for final narrative
Prepared 2026-10-30; current surface/friction outcomes are pending final audited reporting. No unfinished summary is treated as final. Historical numbers below come from completed published reports, not recomputed/reselected outcomes.

POTENTIAL CENTRAL CLAIM
Position-conditioned physical forecasting can separate moving material from known scene interactions, fit material/friction hypotheses from an observed calibration motion, and transfer those hypotheses to different prescribed interactions. The controlled study makes this test concrete with shared contact, consistent mass, known geometry and independent reference checks. Whether it improves accuracy over the strongest rigid/oracle/adapted baselines must be filled from the final fixed-cohort result tables, not inferred from implementation or development success.

A low world error alone is not elasticity identification. Objects with largely rigid motion remain valuable controls, not proof of deformable recovery.

2. MATERIAL UNKNOWN, GEOMETRY KNOWN
Current surface protocol estimates a discrete Young-modulus/friction pair from sparse noisy squeeze positions. Geometry, initial rest grid, constitutive family, density/gravity, contact geometry, contact stiffness/damping law and prescribed tools are known inputs. Supports material/friction fitting under known scene physics; does not establish inferred arbitrary boundary geometry, unknown support positions, unknown gravity, or general unknown force fields. Each object's parameters transfer unchanged to slide/curved/moving-sphere and exploratory edge-drop.
Evidence to cite after completion: original/fine selection JSON, per-object calibration losses, post-fit E-relative and friction-absolute errors, oracle forecasts, grid-edge/coverage diagnostics. No posterior exists in this grid argmin procedure: neither calibration loss nor a narrow score basin is a calibrated credible interval.
Missing ablations: unknown geometry/support placement; independent density/gravity ambiguity; material-family mismatch; truly heterogeneous material; a converged reference posterior and coverage under repeated datasets.

3. POSITION INPUT, NOT MEASURED FORCE INPUT
Supported algorithm claim: predictors receive initial geometry, observed calibration positions, time and prescribed tool position/active tracks. Contact forces are computed by the physical model rather than supplied as measured force labels. This is action-conditioned forecasting when future tool trajectories are provided, not autonomous prediction of unknown future actions. The moving sphere has dynamic state and reacts to contact; prescribed tools do not become inferred dynamic hands.
Evidence: frozen pipeline prepare_object/evaluate and inference.py; fixed protocol input schema; independent observations/selection audit. Material truth is used only for reference/oracle and explicitly post-fit diagnostics, not ordinary candidate selection.
Missing ablation: prediction from image pixels alone, noisy/incomplete tool tracking, unknown initial velocity, unobserved force sources, inferred gravity or external field.

4. REAL COUPLING, LIMITED OBJECT CLASS
Implemented controlled interaction: one deformable FEM block, static known floor/box/sphere obstacles, prescribed tools, and a moving rigid sphere with equal-and-opposite contact reaction, friction and separation. Consistent P1 mass for deformable/native dynamics and exact rigid inertia address mesh-dependent inertial artifacts; exact P1 normal contact integration on planes/boxes plus refined friction/sphere quadrature is checked on development.
Evidence: frozen consistent_mass/clipped_contact sources, force/torque/energy and integration tests, moving_sphere forecast/body-center error and per-case reference qualification. Implementation conservation tests support the solver property; final trajectories support its demonstrated use.
Not demonstrated: two deformable objects, general articulated rigid object scenes, reconstructed hand collision, arbitrary soft topology, or semantic rigid/soft discovery. Rounded capsule support was development-only and not adopted; current stress scenario is sharp edge_drop. Do not call curved sphere obstacle a bowl or container.
