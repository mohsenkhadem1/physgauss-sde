GENERIC SCOPE GUARD AUDIT; NO SCOPE ADOPTED

Runtime guard and independent audit have separate implementations. Both require
an explicit adopted decision artifact, exact scenario partition, frozen
qualification SHA and calibration+primary evidence hashes with strict boolean
pass values. Full4primary requires overall all_gates_pass true. Narrowed3primary
requires overall false, with sharp edge_drop permanently outside primary.

Independent audit additionally binds each scene to deterministic aggregate entry
[scenarios,scene,qualified], preventing unrelated true booleans from authorizing
scenes. It checks primary/companion/dt/halfstep, predictor/contact settings and
all five gate coverage/source/pass flags against protocol. No final protocol or
adoption artifact has been created by this work.

Main and supplemental reports have separate primary/stress/full cohorts.
Independent recomputation checks fixed24or32primary reserved denominators, all40
forecast rows, explicit method failures and paired coverage/gains. Reference
quality never changes primary membership; qualified-only means are secondary.
Supplement source hashes updated after generic wiring. Pre-freeze checklist
continues to fail unresolved actual protocol/evidence as intended.

Temporary fixture tests cover hash-valid false evidence, missing calibration,
conditional rather than adopted decision, unqualified rows retained in24case
primary cohort, and mutation of a reported denominator by quality filtering.
