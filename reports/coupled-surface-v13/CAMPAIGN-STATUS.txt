Completed evidence
The original v13 study is published at dcb64c482fb76ff902fd042703f34a8d89eb498d.
Its 32 reserved forecasts improve world-coordinate error against the rigid
comparison, but its edge reference fails spatial consistency. Those results
and warnings remain unchanged. The extension preservation manifest verifies
405 published v11/v12/v13 files.

Current development work
Clipped triangle contact resolves normal plane/box contact integration;
friction and sphere quadrature have separate refinement checks. The marginal
edge case and failed midpoint-contact trials remain archived. A new prospective
edge scene places the center of mass 12 mm beyond the support edge, and uses
stronger contact stiffness. All 16 endpoint stress tests pass, with maximum
penetration 0.897 mm. Contact, timestep and spatial checks are still underway.
A separate consistent P1 mass candidate is being implemented because the
lumped coarse mesh overestimates rigid rotational inertia. It must pass its
own dynamics and numerical checks before use. No new reserved forecasts exist.

Next decision

then freeze every scene, numerical setting, source, observation and comparator.
Run 2 development and 8 reserved objects under four forecast interactions.
Include rigid, native spring-force adaptation, position baselines, matched

Scope
This is a controlled simulation qualification and cross-interaction study.
It does not yet demonstrate improvement on the archived SoftVTBench or real
PhysTwin datasets. The native spring-force comparison is not the full authors'
PhysTwin method. Raw data and checkpoints remain local. Physics, benchmark
implementation and independent audit are assigned to separate agents.
