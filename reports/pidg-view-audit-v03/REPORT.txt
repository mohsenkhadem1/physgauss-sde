PIDG paper PSNR and off-path camera audit
=======================================
The published AAAI paper Table 1 reports 33.31 dB for Balls Reaction.
Its stated training resolution is 1600x900. No published PSNR typo was
established. The official standalone NCHW evaluator computes standard RGB
PSNR. Its CHW training logger instead averages channel PSNR; this is a
separate convention and not proof that the published table is incorrect.

Both existing matched checkpoints were trained at 320x180. Re-rendering all
38 untouched test views at 1600x900 gives:
                             PIDG        PIDG + frozen material prior
Whole-image PSNR             31.6412     31.6382 dB
Foreground PSNR              22.4114     22.6231 dB
SSIM                         0.97123     0.97189
LPIPS-Alex                   0.03230     0.03235
These are resolution stress tests of the existing models, not a reproduction
of the authors' 1600x900 training. Full results remain in audit.json. Earlier
320px and 640px results have not been replaced.

The integration is our frozen material regularizer inside PIDG, not our full
mechanics/contact/force-inference model. No substantial PSNR advantage is
established by this experiment.

Novel camera experiment
-----------------------
Frozen models, 3 predeclared times (first/middle/last test samples), 7 yaw
offsets (-90,-60,-30,0,30,60,90 degrees), shared camera poses and rendering.
42 images total across both methods. Rotation is about the world Z axis and
world origin. Nonzero camera positions are checked against all released
poses in the manifests. Original test orientations are within about 1.73
degrees of a fitting-camera orientation. Off-path images are visibly much
harder: both methods show fragmented surfaces and floating appearance.
There is no clear qualitative victory for this material-prior integration.

No reference images exist locally for these invented camera/time pairs.
Consequently no PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS or ground-truth error colors are assigned
to them. Comparing to a nearby captured view would be invalid for dynamic
geometry. The contact sheet includes every predeclared view, not selected wins.

Next useful controlled test: obtain original Blender scene assets and rerender
identical states from independent camera trajectories, or use a dataset with
synchronized held-out cameras. Improve view-independent geometry/appearance
alongside the material prior; an inversion penalty alone did not prevent the
observed unseen-view artifacts.

Reproduce with scripts/pidg_comparison/novel_views.py for each selected v02
checkpoint, evaluate.py --render-width 1600 --split test, and audit_views.py.
Raw RGB, per-view predictions and checkpoints remain local under runs/.
