SEPARATE SURFACE-CONTACT EXTENSION: DEVELOPMENT HISTORY
The extension preserves the frozen original study. Its edge initial center moves from x=0.008 to x=0.018 m, giving an 8 mm offset from the narrow support center, and its contact stiffness is 19,200 N/m per 0.0004 m² reference patch. Fixed material-surface midpoint quadrature initially improved contact coverage but did not establish convergence. At 4 versus 6 cells, world trajectory errors remained 7.436/3.759 mm for the two edge materials and 1.684/3.628 mm for curved contact. Edge contact quadrature differences were 4.189 mm (3 to 1.5 mm spacing), 3.053 mm (1.5 to 0.75 mm), and 2.202 mm (0.75 to 0.375 mm). Those failed runs and predeclared plans remain archived. No spatial or integration tolerance was relaxed.

The new implementation clips actual deformed boundary triangles against plane/box interiors and partitions box contact by nearest face. It integrates normal pressure analytically using rest-surface area and original triangle barycentric coordinates. Friction uses refined triangle quadrature; sphere contact uses conservatively accelerated material quadrature. Forces and reaction torques use matching application points. This fixes discontinuous edge sampling without claiming continuous collision detection or eliminating FEM discretization error. A content-hashed immutable C++ source snapshot is compiled locally, with compiler, flags and shared-library digest recorded and checked. There is no silent fallback.

Independent operator auditing passed objectivity, separating-contact, force/torque and passive-friction checks. Development trajectory checks passed friction level 1 versus 2 (maximum 0.0156 mm world RMS) and sphere spacing 1.5 versus 0.75 mm (maximum 0.0215 mm) over both development materials. Full timestep and FEM spatial qualification are pending at this entry; these integration results alone do not validate a reference mesh or justify parameter-inference claims.

CLIPPED MARGINAL-EDGE SPATIAL RESULT
Exact-contact integration and timestep gates pass, but spatial qualification fails. Cells 6 versus 8 edge trajectory RMS is 12.948/7.191 mm for the two development materials, with aligned shape error 3.264/2.932 mm. Refining to 8 versus 10 reduces world errors to 5.223/5.679 mm but leaves shape errors 2.357/2.272 mm. The soft material reaches 1.145 mm penetration at cells8, also failing the fixed 1 mm bound. Curved contact passes at 8 versus 10 (world 0.379/0.917 mm; shape 0.112/0.149 mm). Consequently the current complete reference is not accepted. The initial block center x=0.018 m coincides with the support right edge x=0.018 m; the prior 8 mm center offset still created a marginal tipping configuration. Saved-frame diagnosis shows 10–20 ms differences in reaching 45 degrees and 10 ms differences in floor impact, followed by substantial rotation/deformation disagreement. These outcomes remain a challenging failed stress test, not a successful forecast benchmark.

A new engineering candidate was declared before its outcomes: initial center x=0.03 m (12 mm beyond the support right edge) and contact penalty 76800 N/m per reference patch. The block still overlaps and impacts the narrow support, but its initial center is unambiguously beyond the support. Endpoint feasibility screening includes E=2000/18000 Pa and friction=0/0.8 at cells4/8. This candidate must independently pass contact, timestep and spatial qualification; no tolerance has changed.

INITIAL INERTIAL DISCRETIZATION DIAGNOSTIC
The row-sum lumped tetrahedral mass model exactly preserves total mass and initial center, but not the uniform block's rotational inertia. Diagonal moments exceed analytic values by 50%, 22.22%, 12.5%, 5.56%, 3.125% and 2% at cells 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 10. Fixed tetrahedral diagonals also produce small nonzero off-diagonal inertia entries. This is an additional known source of coarse-mesh rigid-motion bias, separate from contact integration and elastic approximation. It does not invalidate the method's limiting convergence, but cautions against interpreting coarse cells2 parameter fits as unbiased material estimates. The diagnostic does not change the mass matrix or any frozen result.
