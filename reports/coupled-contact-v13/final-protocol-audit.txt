FINAL INDEPENDENT PROTOCOL/EVIDENCE AUDIT: ORIGINAL VERTEX-CONTACT v13

Outcome
The frozen original study completed all 14 objects and56declared cross-interaction
forecasts (development 8, validation 16, reserved 32). Independent reconstruction
passes 3502 checks with zero errors. The supplementary fixed-nominal baseline
passes 1564 checks over all 56 cases. All347archived v12 files retain their hashes.
No source or test-case substitution was needed to obtain these audit passes.
Audit success means recorded calculations/accounting match saved artifacts; it
does NOT mean the underlying reference is spatially accurate.

Reserved evidence: eight objects, four interactions each
Inferred FEM completes 32/32 forecasts with 13.588 mm mean world-coordinate RMSE.
Matched rigid contact completes 32/32 at 20.081 mm; inferred FEM wins 28 and loses 4.
Fixed nominal FEM completes 32/32 at 15.729 mm; inference improves the mean by
2.141 mm (about13.6%), with 25 wins and 7 losses. True-parameter coarse FEM averages
12.047 mm, showing that even known material parameters leave substantial
coarse-grid/contact prediction error.

Native spring-core adaptation completes 20/32reserved forecasts. Its 33.374 mm
mean is conditional on those 20; the paired inferred-FEM gain on the same 20 is
17.762 mm, with 19 wins and 1 loss. Do not compare its conditional mean directly to
FEM's full 32 case mean or label this adaptation official PhysTwin. The twelve
native failures remain in accounting. Position ridge averages 49.630 mm over 32,
but it lacks scene collision geometry; this is a simple contextual comparison,
not an equal-physics baseline.

FEM world-coordinate means by reserved interaction:
slide 10.359 mm; edge-drop 24.263 mm; curved 7.363 mm; moving-sphere 12.367 mm.
Corresponding rigid-aligned shape errors are 1.330,3.021,1.417,1.361 mm. These
separate shape prediction from object placement. The edge case remains the
largest world-position error and has independent spatial-reference concerns.

Numerical qualification and spatial limitation
Joint calibration/forecast numerical gates pass 8/8development,15/16validation,
and 26/32reserved cases. Six reserved forecast references fail declared gates:
test1 slide/curved/moving-sphere exceed timestep discrepancy; test5 edge-drop
exceeds timestep discrepancy; test7 edge-drop exceeds penetration and timestep
limits; test7 moving-sphere exceeds penetration limits. These cases remain in
the full result table; qualified-only means must be explicitly labeled.

More importantly, passing temporal gates DOES NOT establish spatial reference
accuracy. A separate development-only known-parameter body-mesh4-versus6 audit
finds edge-drop trajectory discrepancies 12.13 mm and 29.72 mm on the two materials,
with final orientation differences 57.15 and 105.64 degrees. Curved-contact spatial
differences are 0.872 and 2.497 mm. Vertex sampling of a narrow edge changes contact
and tipping branches as the body mesh changes. A time-converged trajectory may
therefore be an accurate integration of a spatially inadequate contact model.
Neither the 26 temporally qualified reserved cases nor the saved positive-volume
checks justify a blanket continuum-accurate reference claim.

Interpretation
The original study establishes executable nonlinear deformable/rigid coupling,
position-only parameter-grid fitting and controlled cross-interaction transfer
against the declared discrete reference. Parameter fitting has a measurable,
nonuniform benefit over a fixed-parameter model. It does not establish unbiased
material recovery, calibrated uncertainty, a general multi-deformable simulator,
or superiority on SoftVTBench/official PhysTwin. No new external dataset rerun
occurs here; old public benchmark evidence remains preserved separately.

A separately declared surface-quadrature extension is scientifically appropriate.
It must qualify both quadrature and body-resolution consistency on development,
freeze all decisions before fresh reserved data, and report remaining spatial
sensitivity. Its results cannot overwrite or retroactively validate the original
vertex-contact references. Increasing contact stiffness or shifting the edge
geometry must be shared by every new method; matched vertex ablations must
recompute their 35calibration hypotheses under that same new scene.

Audit mechanics
Parameter choices were reconstructed independently from every saved calibration
observation/mask and all hypothesis losses. World/shape/moving-body errors were
recomputed from saved arrays; tetrahedral determinants checked independently;
reference qualification and complete case identities checked. Substep penetration
and minimum-volume logs were threshold-checked but unsaved substeps were not
independently replayed. Uncertainty and inference identifiability remain separate
questions from reproducible scoring.
