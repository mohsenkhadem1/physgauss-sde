Remaining model limits
The model is a research FEM simulator with penalty contact, regularized Coulomb friction and prescribed position/velocity tools. It has no continuous collision detection, self-contact or general deformable-deformable scene solver. Force/torque consistency and passive friction are tested, but those properties alone do not validate arbitrary contact transients. The current work supplies a more carefully qualified simulation benchmark and physical model; it does not by itself demonstrate a Gaussian reconstruction transfer result or superiority on external vision datasets.
