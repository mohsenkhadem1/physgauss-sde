COUPLED CONTACT CAMPAIGN v13

Maximum duration: 12 hours. Prior v11/v12 source and evidence remain frozen.
Raw simulation datasets/checkpoints remain local; publish code, derived metrics,
protocol hashes, videos and failure cases.

Goal: fit unknown response from observed positions during squeezing, then predict
new sliding, edge-drop, curved-obstacle and moving-body interactions from initial
geometry and prescribed future tool motion. Gravity and known scene geometry are
public inputs; true material/friction and future object motion are evaluation-only.

Development: qualify a tetrahedral nonlinear FEM with deforming contact and equal
and opposite reactions on a movable body. Explicitly report penalty penetration,
mesh/time-step sensitivity and failures. Fine reference and coarse predictor
meshes reduce exact discretization matching but do not constitute independent
physical validation when both share the constitutive law and solver family.

Before reserved generation: freeze cohort parameter combinations, interaction
families, measurement noise, observation masks, fitting budgets, selection rules,
metrics and all tested source hashes. Keep development, validation and reserved
objects distinct. Never choose actions/parameters from future prediction errors.

Compare: learned physical parameters, oracle-parameter diagnostic, rigid motion
with matched contact, position regression, and faithful adapted PhysTwin where its
native interface permits. Report coverage and all failures, world-position and
rigid-aligned shape errors, penetration and reaction diagnostics. Preserve prior
benchmark outputs; a new synthetic result is not a revised public-dataset score.

Three agents independently cover physics implementation/qualification, protocol
review, and baseline/native PhysTwin adaptation. Root owns orchestration and
position-only inverse inference. Decisions and rejected changes are recorded.
