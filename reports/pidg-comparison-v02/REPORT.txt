PIDG integration: matched public-scene reconstruction comparison
==============================================================

Completed on GPUs 0-2. This experiment plugs our previously trained, frozen
material-energy network into the authors' PIDG training pipeline. It is a
material-module integration, not the complete contact/force-inference solver.

Public dataset: PIDG balls_reaction (synthetic).
101 fitting images, 11 validation images, 38 final-test images.
All methods: 50,000 initial steps plus 5,000 declared refinement steps.
The refinement strength was chosen on validation before its test evaluation.
Initial results and both validation candidates are retained, including losses.

Average final-test scores             PIDG       Integration
RGB PSNR at 320px (higher better)      49.54      48.16 dB
Foreground PSNR at 320px               39.62      38.19 dB
SSIM at 320px                         0.99872    0.99826
LPIPS at 320px (lower better)          0.000423   0.000726
RGB PSNR at 640px                      34.48      34.41 dB
Foreground PSNR at 640px               25.28      25.41 dB
Sampled deformation-map inversions     0.1336%     0.0000%

The hybrid wins whole-image PSNR on 6/38 frames and foreground PSNR on 5/38.
It does not beat PIDG's average rendering quality. Higher-resolution rendering
is nearly tied overall. The geometry diagnostics improve, but they are not
physical-accuracy measurements against known material properties or forces.

The selected 640px crops show genuine local improvements:
- Frame 0081, green ball: RGB RMSE 0.06839 -> 0.04868 (28.8% lower).
- Frame 0129, blue ball:  RGB RMSE 0.06564 -> 0.04827 (26.5% lower).
These were selected after evaluation. They do not establish overall superiority.

Figures: figures/selected-comparison.png, error-maps.png,
all-frame-gains.png, and failure.png. Identical cameras, ground truth, display
ranges and crop coordinates are used. No generated or cosmetically enhanced
predictions are included. Pixel error maps use one shared clipped color scale.

Reproduction details: protocol.json, run-provenance.json, validation-results.json,
results.json, final-audit.json, and scripts/pidg_comparison/README.txt.
The earlier v01 experiment is preserved beside this report. Raw data, full
reconstruction checkpoints, and individual rendered frame arrays stay local.
