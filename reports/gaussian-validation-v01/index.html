Held-out gap prediction improved. Physical uncertainty remains unreliable.

The25D RGB/RGBD baselines improve the1–2s withheld gap. Only the depth-assisted chart model improves the2–3s future-window mean on this validation set, by2.93%; RGB-only future prediction remains worse than static. Surface/force constraints make hypotheses valid but do not establish parameter recovery or calibration.

All models use the same frozen neural Green teacher and known cuboid reference. Runtime is an observation encoder plus sampled physical hypotheses and a neural decoder: no FEM or per-video parameter optimization. RGBD explicitly uses measured depth and is a separate track.

Scope and reproducibility

  • Chart training uses50% exact≤1s prefixes and50% random frame dropout. The preserved25D baseline uses random dropout only; the forecast difference cannot be attributed to the chart alone.
  • The chart is specific to this known cuboid and declared force families. It does not demonstrate arbitrary COLMAP geometry or generalized3D boundary fields.
  • The chart density is16D intrinsic coefficients plus discrete categories, including nuisance padding. Its noninvertible physical mapping has no25D Lebesgue density claim.
  • Intervals and categorical probabilities remain uncalibrated development estimates. No test-based tuning is included.
