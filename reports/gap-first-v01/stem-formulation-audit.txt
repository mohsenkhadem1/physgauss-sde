COMPLIANT-STEM FORMULATION AUDIT

Model: uniform Euler–Bernoulli beam of lengthL, bending rigidityEI, base lateral
spring stiffnesskb. Base translation is free/compliant but base rotation is
constrained. Tip is otherwise free; small-deflection, static linear response.
For a transverse point forceF applied at s, displacement at x is
 u(x;s)=F G(x,s),
 G(x,s)=1/kb + min(x,s)^2 [3max(x,s)-min(x,s)]/(6EI).
Units: G m/N; EI N m²; kb N/m. G is reciprocal in x,s.

Let total tip complianceC=1/kb+L³/(3EI), bending fractionalpha=(L³/3EI)/C.
Two alternatives alpha=.8 andalpha=.2 with sameC give identical tip response.
These are intrinsic observational alternatives, not forward-solver failures.

DISTINGUISH TWO DIFFERENT HALF-HEIGHT EXPERIMENTS
1. Keep original force at tip s=L and observe x=L/2:
   G(L/2,L)/C=(1-alpha)+(5/16)alpha.
   A80/20:0.450; B20/80:0.8625.
2. Apply a NEW force at s=L/2 and measure there x=L/2:
   G(L/2,L/2)/C=(1-alpha)+(1/8)alpha.
   A80/20:0.300; B20/80:0.825.
The user/root .300/.825 proposal is correct for experiment2.
Loadlocation and observationlocation must remain separate in code/captions.

OBSERVABILITY
With known F, parameterize beta=(1/kb,1/EI). Each measurement has row
F[1,g(x,s)] with g=min²(3max-min)/6. Two measurements have rank2 iff
nonzero forces and distinctg. Repeated tip observations cannot distinguish
support/material, even at more camera angles. Interiorlook orhalfheightpush
adds rank when the displacement component is visible. With unknown originalF,
shape information alone retains common(EI,kb,F) scaling ambiguity. Knownprobe
anchors absolute scale. Here primaryF isknown, so isolate support/material.
A held third-location response must not also be acquired/evaluated astraining.
