Independent prospective architecture/protocol proposal — v16
Status: design proposal, NOT a method/data freeze. No v16 reserved truth generated or read by this audit. DIRECTION.txt reviewed; v15 frozen results remain separate immutable evidence.

Falsifiable first question
Can ONE trained bulk constitutive law, reused at material quadrature points, compose with a fixed explicit compliant attachment law to predict new geometry/material/support combinations under unseen prescribed positions? Then, with shared weights frozen, can sparse permitted observations identify useful scene latents and reduce held-response error? A positive answer requires transferable stress/deformation/reaction predictions, not a more accurate image warp or memorized scene decoder.
Stage A must remain bilateral compliant attachments. If the interface is still analytic Robin elasticity, call it composition WITH an analytic interface, not a learned general contact law. Learning a scalar Robin coefficient alone does not establish a new shared nonlinear interface mechanism. Unilateral contact/friction and moving rigid bodies are later bounded stages; no friction detour is needed to answer the first question.

Constitutive teacher and learned law
Physics agent proposes Mooney–Rivlin and Yeoh reference families, general isoparametric Q2 geometries and the existing equilibrium solver. Use both teachers in training, with ranges that visibly differ at held strains after matching their small-strain tangent. This first study tests composition within seen constitutive families, not generalization to an unseen family. A held-family challenge can be separately declared later; do not call parameter interpolation a new-law result.
Architecture inputs are deformation gradient F and scene material latent z_M(X), evaluated in material coordinates. Shared weights never see a scene ID or held trajectory index. Objectivity W(QF,z)=W(F,z), material isotropy W(FQ,z)=W(F,z), W(I,z)=0 and P(I,z)=0 must hold for every admitted latent, not just the average training latent. Stress comes from the same energy derivative used by the mechanics layer. A nonnegative energy or differentiable stress alone does not prove convexity, strong ellipticity, coercivity or solver stability.
If using an admissible analytic backbone plus neural residual, constrain the residual/conditioning so latent modulation cannot destroy those intended properties. Subtracting a general linear P0:(F−I) to cancel reference stress may break objectivity; use a justified invariant normalization. Convexity in invariants is not automatically convexity/polyconvexity in the needed deformation variables. State the architecture's actual guarantee and separately report sampled rank-one/tangent tests. Random Hessian checks are diagnostics, not mathematical proofs.
Keep positive-J guards and a declared admissible strain/latent envelope. Test pure rotation, near-identity strain, uniaxial/shear/biaxial loading, volumetric compression and extrapolated latent bounds. Check energy/stress/tangent finite differences and equilibrium reaction-energy work consistency. Small-strain agreement alone is insufficient to train/test nonlinear law transfer.

Geometry and solver reuse
The old TensorFixture assumes rectangular reference widths and hardcoded dimensions; the planned general isoparametric adapter is a genuine geometry extension needing its own reference-Jacobian/physical-gradient/volume/surface-area validation. A curved stress-free reference shape is permissible, but must not acquire spurious initial stress from mapping. Run a constant-physical-deformation affine patch test on every mapped geometry, rigid rotation objectivity, reference stress/energy zero and consistent traction/attachment integration. Surface normals, attachment locations, query coordinates and wrench origins must all use the same mapped geometry.
Reuse the Newton/global equilibrium algorithm without claiming prior mesh qualification automatically transfers to a new constitutive tangent or geometry. Qualify mesh and integration order at material/geometry/support extremes and held-action envelopes BEFORE reserved truth generation. Distinguish geometry-map quadrature, constitutive approximation and solver residual error. Compare reaction curves as well as displacement; numerical stress errors can remain when shape looks correct.

Bounded scene split proposal
Represent the first factor lattice as three geometry instances G0/G1/G2 × two bulk families M0/M1 × two support layouts B0/B1 × two heterogeneous-field patterns H0/H1:24 cells. These are actual geometry instances, not necessarily three independently sampled shape populations. Hold out six whole four-factor cells: for geometry index g∈{0,1,2} and family m∈{0,1}, reserve B=(g+m)mod2 and H=gmod2. Train on the other18 cells. This preserves every individual factor and every pairwise combination in training while withholding specific joint compositions. Verify that property mechanically from the declared manifest before any data generation.
Proposed cohorts:18 training scenes (one initial draw per retained cell), four development scenes for architecture/range/tolerance choice, four independent calibration scenes for discrepancy/interval calibration, twelve reserved scenes. Reserved split C has the six withheld lattice cells; split G has six draws on two entirely untrained geometry instances (three each), with both material/support classes represented by a predeclared balanced schedule. All reserved continuous material/fixture parameters and field amplitudes are new draws; avoid frame-level splits. If more training parameter draws are needed, add them only before freeze and report the true scene count.
New geometry instances within the same parameterized block/taper/arch family establish shape-parameter transfer, not unseen topology or arbitrary shapes. Train/dev/calibration/test must share no realized state trajectories. Calibration scenes may set a prespecified discrepancy/coverage adjustment, not choose the network, probe policy or winning architecture. Reserved scenes cannot be replaced for failures or low performance.
This is a proposed matrix: exact geometry dimensions/maps, material parameter ranges, field patterns, seed values and action arrays must be set after the bounded development qualification and BEFORE test generation. No late changes from reserved responses.

Actions and identification
Use a small fixed family of bilateral position/rotation commands: weak initial deformation plus bending, shear, stretch/compression and unloading/reset commands. Reserve different directions/combinations/amplitudes for four final response commands. Choose the finite safe amplitude range so deformations and between-law differences exceed both observation noise and measured numerical discrepancy. An unloading endpoint in a memoryless hyperelastic model is a path-independence/solver check, not viscoelastic hysteresis evidence.
Treat tool poses as input and reaction wrench as output. V uses geometry/visible observations only; VF additionally observes reactions under the same commanded poses and matched acquisition budgets. Known density/gravity, if used, are separate model inputs with units; do not smuggle true forces into control inputs.
Preserve a common-scale ambiguity control: with no external loads, scaling all bulk and bilateral fixture energy coefficients together leaves equilibrium displacement unchanged and scales reactions. An extra view cannot identify that scale. Unknown independent material/fixture ratios create further confounding; do not overparameterize elementwise latents without evidence. Report predictive response error before asserting unique latent recovery.

Required matched comparisons
1. Oracle-latent learned shared energy: truth latents supplied, shared weights frozen. Tests the forward law/composition separately from inverse estimation.
2. Inferred-latent learned energy: optimize/ensemble only the declared scene latents using permitted prefix evidence; no shared-weight fine-tuning on reserved scenes.
3. Matched analytic-family FEM: same geometry/interface, observations and latent capacity, with the correct MR/Yeoh family option. Its oracle version is a reference/ceiling, not a baseline the learned law is expected to outperform. Its inferred version is the strong scientific comparator.
4. Fixed-family/nominal FEM: deliberately single-family or fixed parameters, explicitly labeled misspecified. Beating it alone does not establish advantage over analytic physical inference.
5. Geometry-conditioned direct displacement/reaction network: same train scenes, public coordinates/geometry encoding, tool poses, oracle/inferred latent interface and output supervision. A fixed-reference trajectory model cannot silently stand in for a geometry-transfer baseline. Existing gaussian_operator/modal_operator remain unmodified historical controls.
Match train-only label access and fitting budgets; disclose if energy/stress supervision is used to pretrain the law but not available to an inverse method. Never give true test material/support labels to only the learned model. Reaction prediction and geometric prediction are separate outcomes. Report best rigid-motion control for world-vs-shape interpretation.

Stages and stop gates
A0: analytic teacher + general geometry verification; no training claim until objectivity/reference state/derivative/patch/wrench tests pass.
A1: qualified training/development response bank and meaningful nonlinear separation of teacher families; fix primary action/latent ranges and scene splits.
A2: oracle-latent forward composition. Stop/promote based on independently qualified response errors versus matched analytic FEM and direct neural control. A failure here is not repaired by more sophisticated posterior inference.
A3: freeze shared weights; inferred latents on allowed prefixes. Compare V and VF, and a small numerical/ensemble reference wherever feasible. Parameter ambiguity remains a result, not a failed optimization to hide.
A4: separate calibration cohort fixes numerical/model discrepancy and a declared uncertainty calibration procedure. Report latent versus noisy-observation coverage separately; v15 showed that point gains and lower CRPS can coexist with severe interval undercoverage.
A5: one/two additional observations with camera-independent fixed two-bend, camera-conditioned fixed, random, visual-uncertainty and physical-risk policies. This follows A2/A3, not simultaneous open-ended training. Design actions remain separate from reserved response actions, and all forecasts commit before target scoring.
B: only after A, one bounded explicit normal-contact scene with independent reaction/nonpenetration checks. Bilateral springs may carry tension; nonadhesive contact may not. Friction requires tangential history and dissipative stick/slip evolution, not just an inequality or an energy potential. Defer adhesion/soft-soft contact/viscoelastic memory.

Claim ledger
Potentially supported after A2: shared-law composition across held factor combinations and specified new geometries, with known oracle latents. Not yet scene identification.
Potentially supported after A3: predictive identification from permitted observations with globally frozen laws; not necessarily unique material/fixture parameters.
Potentially supported after A5: new data improves independent response predictions. Active superiority requires beating strong global fixed probes as well as random controls; v15's VF benefit was largely explained by a constant two-bend schedule.
Not supported by Stage A: learned general contact, friction, momentum transfer among moving bodies, arbitrary topology, unseen constitutive-family transfer, anisotropy, real-camera reconstruction or improved external-benchmark performance.
Current v14/v15 reports and raw datasets stay untouched/local. Any retention check on old datasets must use their actual available outputs/modalities and be reported separately; no invented wrench/contact labels.

Reporting/qualification floor
For every scene/action report total and rigid-removed deformation, reaction/torque, solver failures, positive-J margins, and mesh/quadrature discrepancy. All declared failures remain in denominators. Learned energies must not receive looser reference gates than analytic baselines. Posterior mean responses are statistical estimates and need not satisfy equilibrium; MAP/sample rendering must use the same physical state and law, with no image-only motion correction. Geometry-aware Gaussian appearance is optional and secondary until mechanical composition works.
