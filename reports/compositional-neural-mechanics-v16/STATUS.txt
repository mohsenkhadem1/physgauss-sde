COMPOSITIONAL NEURAL MECHANICS — DEVELOPMENT MILESTONE

Goal remains active. This is not the reserved benchmark result or a completed paper.

Implemented:
- Shared objective bulk energy law with exact common-stiffness scaling.
- Shared convex radial bilateral attachment energy; distinct from unilateral contact.
- Mapped-geometry Q2 nonlinear equilibrium solver, controlled by tool positions/orientations.
- Implicit parameter gradients and conditional bounded MAP; optional measured wrenches.
- Custom direct neural predictor with matched physical descriptors and frozen-law fingerprints.
- Resumable, source-pinned data and baseline runners; raw datasets stay local.

Evidence so far:
- All 15 development teacher responses have a passing mesh-refinement pair; original failures retained.
- Original attachment model failed at large slips. Failed wide random-start training is retained too.
- Warm-start range repair independently achieves 0.629% aggregate coupon force error in its widened range; saturated-family extrapolation error remains 6.533%.
- Coupled learned n4 prediction against n6 teacher: mean field RMS 0.02446 mm, maximum 0.08399 mm over 15 development responses, oracle physical descriptors.
- Same-mesh difficult stretch example improves from 4.065 mm to 0.07869 mm after attachment repair.
- Controlled three-parameter fit: shape-only observations cannot identify common material/support stiffness scale. Adding wrench observations reduces learned held-out force error from 0.05370 N to 0.000911 N; shape errors remain about 7 micrometres.
- The mechanism fit uses known nuisance parameters and same-mesh analytic truth; it is not full unknown-scene inference or a calibrated posterior.
- 57 integration tests pass; one tensor-to-float diagnostic warning remains.
- All 8,028 preserved prior tracked files remain byte-identical.

Running/next:

Force errors worsened for one object and improved for the other; no global-optimum or posterior claim.

The variance policy selected stretch for both objects and has no demonstrated advantage over that fixed choice. All eight fits succeeded; nominal 90% marginal coordinate coverage after stretch is 85.26%, with correlated coordinates and only two exposed objects.
