Prospective direct-baseline and input-fairness audit

Primary first comparison: oracle-latent forward prediction on withheld factor compositions, separately on new geometry. Follow with frozen-law scene-latent inference from noisy surface material-point coordinates, separately positions only and positions plus controller wrench. Neither is end-to-end RGB-D vision; visibility and tracking must be an explicit later observation stage.

Minimal strong direct baseline: a small DeepSets geometry encoder over a fixed public reference-surface/material-point sampling, mean/max pooling, and a per-query MLP displacement decoder. Supply point coordinates/normals, geometry dimensions, normalized boundary patch weights/locations, controller pose commands, material descriptors and interface descriptors. Encode each interface with a shared patch encoder and pool its features rather than relying on case IDs. A separate global head predicts both controller force/torque vectors in the same declared frames. Use residual displacement output, not absolute world coordinates. Include identity/rest examples and multiple declared command amplitudes in training. A fixed training budget with development-only stopping/checkpoint choice must be declared before reserved evaluation; a tiny underfit baseline should not carry a general architecture superiority claim.

For fair access to prior constitutive supervision, give this direct model frozen-law fingerprints evaluated at a fixed public set of strain tensors and slip vectors (energies/stresses and interface energy/traction). These probes must be declared before reserved scenes, cover stretch/compression/shear and displacement magnitudes, and never be optimized from reserved error. Both oracle models receive identical latent descriptors. In inferred-latent comparison, give both forward models the same estimated latents initially, to isolate forward approximation from inference. A separately fitted direct-model latent estimator is an additional comparison, with the same observations and permitted iterations, not a replacement of that controlled comparison.

Fingerprint access is a useful shared-law prior, not a proof of identical information: finite fingerprints can omit details available to the mechanics solver's full law. Report coupon and scene supervision budgets separately (numbers of energy, stress, traction and scene response labels), and describe this as a prior-informed direct baseline. If practical, additionally train its material/patch embedding to predict the same coupon labels; no extra reserved labels. True-family analytic FEM with oracle parameters is the exact-family ceiling and should be reported independently of misspecified Neo-Hookean or linear-attachment controls.

Report by dataset cohort and action family: displacement, rigid-aligned deformation, reactions, numerical failures and fixed denominators. No scene-specific fine tuning of shared law weights. Geometry/material/interface oracle descriptors are only available to the oracle branch. Fixed stiffness-scale ambiguity in zero-gravity prescribed-position mechanics must be respected rather than fabricated by approximate latent scaling.

Independent implicit-gradient source audit: R(u,z)=0; H^T lambda=L_u; gradient=L_z-lambda^T R_z is correctly implemented, including direct wrench dependence. Free-DOF restriction is correct for latent-independent fixed values; latent-dependent boundary values require lifting. Tangent transpose consistency is checked on one deterministic direction, which is a useful diagnostic but not exhaustive matrix verification. Gates are absolute/normalized numerical tolerances and require scale awareness in production. No Newton iteration graph is differentiated.
