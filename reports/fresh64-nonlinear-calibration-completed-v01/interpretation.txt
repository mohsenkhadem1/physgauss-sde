This is a controlled static reference problem with known geometry, force and support locations and two unknown stiffnesses. It does not establish image-based physical inference, future-motion prediction, arbitrary boundaries/contact/gravity or uncertainty-guided action superiority. The normal probe repeats the original load with independent noise; the axial probe is a different fixed load. No adaptive action policy was tested. Raw observations, physical parameters, posterior arrays and images remain local.
