Metric interpretation

Prediction errors are coordinate RMSE, not vector RMS. Intervals are coordinate marginals, not simultaneous trajectories.

force_profile_rmse_N_per_m evaluates a normalized one-dimensional Gaussian longitudinal load-basis profile multiplied by inferred force amplitudes. It is a compact localization/amplitude proxy. On the stepped side surface, changing transverse area means this is not the exact surface-integrated physical traction profile. No frozen values or inference routines are changed; use the name normalized longitudinal load-basis profile error in reporting.

The five tau values form an equally weighted finite prior; endpoint mass is already 40 percent under that prior. A large endpoint mass alone is not a convergence failure. The variance-matched Gaussian has exactly E[tau squared]=1.55 times the base covariance.
