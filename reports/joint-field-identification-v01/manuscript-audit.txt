ACTIONABLE CORRECTIONS
1. Numerical covariance claim: main.tex says calculations use a Joseph update. This is true for finite inference.py, but continuous.py:282 uses K - V^T V followed by symmetrization. Qualify the sentence to the finite reference, and describe continuous conditioning accurately. Do not change frozen continuous source. Existing Gaussian-identity unit tests cover the formula, but cancellation at high signal/noise remains a numerical caveat.
2. Continuous policy scope: explicitly say continuous SMC re-evaluates the initial and acquired observations chosen by the finite-dictionary planner. continuous_campaign.py does not perform continuous-posterior acquisition optimization. Continuous inference/calibration comparisons cannot be described as an SMC-designed active policy.
3. Symmetry wording: replace 'exact likelihood symmetry' with 'conditional observation-map symmetry' or 'unknown-load displacement symmetry.' The marginalized likelihood with fixed amplitude prior is not invariant under stiffness scaling. The following sentences and numerical example correctly distinguish fixed and co-scaled priors.
4. Temporal scope: eight correlated quasistatic loading times are not a dynamic initial-value problem. Current text largely respects this; keep 'changed-load prediction' and 'hidden displacement' wording. Do not relabel results as future inertial motion, impacts, temporal forecasting, neural ODE/SDE, flow matching, or RGB generation.
5. Historical-result traceability: numerical contact/public/cube results need explicit archived report identifiers in supplement or artifact inventory. Current section correctly separates those adapters from the new marginalized posterior; do not merge their numbers into a single-method leaderboard. This audit did not independently recompute the historical aggregate numbers.
6. Mean modulus: m0 is the volume-centered mean LOG modulus; exp(m0) is its geometric mean, not arithmetic mean E. Use 'baseline/geometric-mean modulus' for the 8–32 kPa bound if exact language matters.
7. MASIV bibliography title currently omits 'MASIV:' prefix found in the primary arXiv title. Authors and venue are correct; adding the prefix is a minor bibliographic improvement.

- Calinon is relevant as frame-conditioned interaction modeling, not as a proof of unknown boundary recovery. Rogers 2020 is a direct precedent for joint input/state/parameter Bayesian inference. NGFF and the active-PhysTwin project must remain acknowledged.
