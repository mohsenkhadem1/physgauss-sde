FINAL IMPLEMENTATION AUDIT — FROZEN v12 PRIMARY METHOD

Scope and independence
This review checks input isolation, frozen source integrity, failure handling,
and physical interpretations of the saved regression predictions. It does not
select model settings using benchmark errors. No frozen primary source was
changed. Untouched-confirmation execution was still in progress during review;
its final completion and metric accounting belong to final-audit.json.

Source and test checks
All 14 SHA256 entries in method-freeze.json match their current files, including
input adapters, primary predictors, FEM/base runners, and scoring code.
40 tests pass across object-frame, prefix isolation, input isolation, static
scene, reduced rollout, nested temporal resolution, and elastic-scene modules.
Test output: runs/contact-benchmark-v12/final-model-audit-tests.txt.

Information boundaries
The primary prediction entry rejects an evaluation dictionary in either full
or validation payload. Object-frame calibration uses only allowed observed
positions and future prescribed controls. Candidate validation receives a
separately fitted shortened-prefix FEM and recalibrated shortened payload;
reserved prefix targets are used only for selection scoring. The selected
adapter is then recalibrated using the full allowed prefix.

SoftVT scene construction reads initial rigid poses and public asset geometry;
future rigid trajectories, material labels, force readings and future target
geometry are not predictor inputs. Poisoning tests cover those boundaries.
Using explicit scene geometry is extra information relative to the old v11
method, so the rigid_scene_state baseline is necessary for a fair comparison.

Failure behavior
Active-frame nonfinite FEM predictions cause object-frame adaptation to reject
rather than fit around missing frames. Inactive inherited failures remain NaN
and are listed in metadata; a successful independent free-motion contact rollout
may replace them. Failed validation predictions receive infinite error, not
silent frame omission. The main experiment retains the inherited prediction
when calibration/contact construction fails and records unavailability.

The scorer reports failed-frame counts alongside errors over valid frames. A
conditional valid-frame error must not be described as complete-cohort accuracy
without its completion count. The planned final verifier compares archived
prediction finiteness with those counts. Retaining a failed base as fallback
preserves accounting, not guaranteed accurate prediction.

What "preserving deformation" does and does not mean
The pose-reset adapter is a rigid transformation of each inherited FEM shape;
pairwise distances are preserved. The residual adapter intentionally CHANGES
shape using a learned, bounded local displacement correction. It removes a
best-fit rigid component but imposes no elastic equilibrium or element-volume
constraint. Thus it cannot be described as preserving the FEM's physical
admissibility merely because it is expressed in material/object coordinates.

The primary reduced contact solver applies rigid rotations/translations after
separation, preserving the shape at that instant. It does NOT predict elastic
relaxation or impact deformation. The separate scene_fem diagnostic supplies
elastic contact response, but is not the frozen primary method.

Actionable geometric-feasibility finding
An independent local-volume audit reconstructed the same FEM tetrahedra and
computed det(Ds)/det(Dm) on saved predictions for all 43 regression cases. The
inherited v11 predictions have no inverted tetrahedra. The primary corrected
predictions have inverted tetrahedra in 16/43 sequences: two PhysTwin stretching
cases and 14 SoftVT episodes. The affected cases and per-frame accounting are
recorded in shape-jacobian-audit.json. Very thin reference tetrahedra can amplify
reported determinant ratios; a negative determinant still means local inversion.

This finding does not invalidate the point-position error measurements, but it
precludes calling the corrected output a physically admissible elastic state,
a non-self-intersecting mesh, or a collision-safe digital twin. The cause is the
unconstrained empirical deformation residual; later rigid contact motion cannot
repair those element inversions. Keep all numerical benchmark results unchanged
and disclose this limitation. Future work should constrain/project residuals
onto feasible geometry before treating them as states for physical simulation.
Positive local Jacobians alone would also not certify global nonintersection.

Contact limitations
Primary contact uses static layered heightfields, an approximate nearest raster
query, sequential impulses and vertical position correction. Vertical walls,
general undercuts and two-way motion of other objects are absent. A detached
stationary prefix hypothesis initializes zero velocity by design; this is a
conservative heuristic, not measured contact state or calibrated uncertainty.
The multiple-object coupled qualification and elastic-contact study are separate
controlled diagnostics, not capabilities demonstrated by the primary benchmark.

Recommendation
Publish the measured position-prediction gains with these capability boundaries.
Preserve the frozen method and previous evidence. Next prioritize feasible
residual geometry and consistent friction/contact resolution, rather than
claiming physically valid material identification or fully coupled contact.

Subsequent separate safeguard

guard; it does not alter the frozen method audited above. See
feasibility-decision.txt and feasibility-development.json for its fixed rule and
