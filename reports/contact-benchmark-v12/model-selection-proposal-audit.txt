EXPLORATORY MODEL-SELECTION PROPOSAL — AUDIT ONLY, NOT IMPLEMENTED

Recommendation
Conditionally proceed as a separately frozen post-audit robustness study if the
coordinator accepts the information-boundary requirements below. Preserve every
original and guarded benchmark result. There is no untouched same-domain
PhysTwin case left; results cannot be advertised as fresh confirmation or a
predeclared primary improvement. No new fitting or selector implementation was
performed for this audit.

Scientific rationale
Refitting the field-free model addresses the observed compensation problem:
removing a term after joint fitting is not equivalent to learning without it.
A rigid hypothesis is also justified because many SoftVT trajectories contain
little deformation. However, a rigid model winning a short prefix does not prove
the material is rigid; the observed excitation may simply be uninformative.
The field-free model still contains the old reversible tangential support term,
so this study would not establish general contact-conditioned boundary physics.

Required information boundaries
Do NOT score the output of existing select_adapter on its own validation frames:
that API returns a selected adapter refit using the full prefix, which includes
those targets. Instead construct candidate predictions using only the separate
70-percent-prefix fit and correctly shortened/recalibrated payload. Generate
all guarded/contact candidate forecasts for the remaining observed prefix, then
score that reserved interval. Select the energy family and adapter configuration
jointly, or use an explicitly nested independent split. Joint finite candidate
selection is simpler; disclose the increased validation search budget.

After selecting a configuration, recalibrate/refit using the full allowed prefix
and forecast the true suffix. No full-prefix fitting residual or suffix accuracy
may influence the choice. Full-prefix field-free fits can be precomputed for
all cases, but their files must be inaccessible to validation selection.
Keep only the prescribed positions, initial geometry and permitted prefix motion
as inputs. Manual markers, force labels and future object/rigid poses remain
excluded. All validation candidates receive the same initial scene geometry.

Candidate and fallback rules to freeze
Use the original current-energy family, a genuinely refit field-free family,
and a calibrated rigid hypothesis. Keep the same data cuts, iterations, feasible
starts, material basis and contact settings. Existing regression/current base
settings were checked and match: field/contact enabled; PhysTwin nonconvex mesh
and six material coefficients; SoftVT one coefficient.

Apply each candidate's local-volume guard BEFORE contact and velocity derivation,
including inside validation. Each FEM candidate uses its OWN feasible inherited
FEM anchor. A valid rigid candidate must use an independently feasible rigid
anchor; failure of an unrelated FEM must not invalidate it. Preserve explicit
failure counts and assign incomplete validation forecasts infinite loss rather
than scoring selected finite frames. Define the infinity/tie policy in advance:
finite beats failed; all failed remains failed; less than two-percent gain keeps
the current guarded family. Within-family ties prefer simpler correction.

If adapter choices are optimized jointly, define "current guarded" precisely as
the best permitted current-family validation candidate; do not quietly compare
against an uncalibrated version. Save every candidate's validation error and
feasibility result, not only the winner. No per-dataset post-hoc switching based
on the observed benchmark results.

Practical integration issue
The old calibrated_rigid helper eagerly reads data['evaluation']['points'][0]
even when support_geometry is available, and PhysTwin output is dense support
geometry rather than the internal nodal mesh. It is therefore unsuitable for the
isolated guarded selector as written. Implement a small payload-only nodal rigid
adapter from initial nodes and prefix calibration; do not pass evaluator truth
to work around the API. A rigid candidate's constant shape is a hypothesis, not
learned elasticity. Reuse scene construction across validation candidates.

Compute estimate, not a guarantee
All148 sequences comprise140SoftVT and8PhysTwin. One new full plus one shortened
field-free fit per sequence means296 new FEM jobs; existing native baselines need
no retraining. Recorded completed fit-plus-predict wall times in the current run:
                         median seconds    mean seconds
SoftVT full                   116.4             161.7
SoftVT shortened               59.9             117.1
PhysTwin full                 322.2             327.2
PhysTwin shortened            192.7             234.3
These imply approximately8worker-hours at medians or12.1worker-hours at observed
means, before selector/contact evaluation and audits. They are worker wall-time
estimates, not CPU-core hours. Eight comparable CPU workers suggest roughly1–2
hours of ideal fit throughput; reserve several hours for long tails, different
field-free optimizer behavior, selection, rendering and verification. Existing
jobs reached about37minutes; retain the already declared bounded timeout policy.
The field-free landscape may converge differently, so a pilot on development
cases should establish feasibility before committing all296 jobs. Keep GPU3 and
unrelated jobs untouched. Do not spend the remaining budget on native retraining.

Limits and stop criteria
Stop or retain an explicit incomplete study if field-free fitting/validation is
systematically infeasible or the time budget cannot support full cohort coverage.
Do not lower geometry thresholds or drop hard cases to obtain favorable means.
A prefix-selected mixture may improve robustness, but neither calibrated material
inference, active probing, unseen-force-family generalization nor a novel contact
solver follows from it. Results across all148 would remain exploratory secondary
comparisons, with the frozen original evidence preserved.
