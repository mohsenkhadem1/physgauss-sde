PhysTwin nonconvex pruning, with J=det(Ds*inverse(Dm)). Re-tetrahedralizing deformed
points can hide inversion and is forbidden. Validate exact input node identities.

the residual-corrected geometry. A predeclared original solver bound J>=0.03 is
preferable to a target-selected cutoff. Alpha=0 must recover the anchor exactly.
If the anchor is invalid/nonfinite, record an unrepaired failure; do not remesh or
silently omit elements. A feasible endpoint alone is not global injectivity.
Check all elements of every frame (including prefix and final outputs). Proper
rigid transformations preserve J; test this numerically. Apply before downstream
contact rollout/velocity initialization and rerun unchanged contact rather than
blending final trajectories with incompatible contact states after the fact.
Record per-sequence original/repaired minimum J, inverted count, altered frame
fraction, alpha values, correction RMS/max, and temporal jump/velocity changes.
Per-frame clipping may create temporal nonsmoothness even if all volumes pass.
Positive J is a local orientation/volume condition, not collision freedom,
noninterpenetration, physical equilibrium, constitutive validity or posterior
calibration. Preserve separate contact and time-step convergence limitations.
