FINAL INDEPENDENT PHYSICS AUDIT — V12
===================================
Scope: completed43-case regression cohort; confirmation is audited separately as
it becomes available. Frozen model was not edited during this audit.

What improved, and what it supports
----------------------------------
Regression SoftVT coordinate RMSE improves26.967->24.184mm, with40/40 cases
improving relative to v11. Added initial scene geometry and pose conditioning
produce measurable practical gains. Improvement is largest in object-task1
(6.705mm mean) and smallest in spatial-task2(0.816mm), whose absolute error
remains53.423mm. A residual contact geometry/attachment failure remains.

The geometry-matched rigid_scene_state baseline averages23.524mm, better than
primary24.184mm, despite primary winning23/40 individual comparisons. Primary
has lower rigid-aligned shape RMSE1.616mm vs rigid2.048mm. These are complementary
results: shape correction helps, but elastic complexity has not established the
best overall position prediction. Original PhysTwin adapted baseline39.351mm
and geometry-matched adapted baseline37.236mm are worse on this regression set.
Additional geometry must remain explicit in comparisons with original baselines.

Regression PhysTwin marker error improves18.332->14.452mm across3/3 cases, but
official PhysTwin13.155mm remains better on each case. Marker error is not the
same as SoftVT coordinate RMSE; do not pool metrics across datasets.

Contact and detachment scope
----------------------------
All40/40 regression SoftVT prefixes classify as kinematically_attached. Therefore
contact_scene and contact_scene_state coincide on this cohort. These results do
not demonstrate early slip/detachment discrimination. The state estimator is a
prefix classifier, not a predictive frictional grasp model.

The benchmark primary is split: corrected FEM-based shape while attached,
rigid-pose contact integration after separation, frozen release shape. Explicit
scene meshes are static; they do not react. Vertical layered queries support
landing but omit side-wall collision, undercuts and full signed-distance contact.
Projection removes residual penetration. This is not momentum-conserving coupled
soft-rigid contact, IPC, or a differentiable contact-material inference method.

Spatial task2 demo0 visually demonstrates the remaining failure: recorded object
ends left of the target plate, but predicted object lands on plate. Correct target
support cannot compensate for missed future slip/drop. Initial-object min-z floor
also differs from actual recorded support height in that case. Data conditioning
is insufficient for some future interactions; no accuracy metric should imply
that all physical events were inferred.

Physical admissibility of corrected geometry
-------------------------------------------
The saved shape-jacobian-audit.json reports inverted original-mesh tetrahedra in
16/43 primary regression sequences and805/2149 evaluated suffix frames. This
includes double_stretch_sloth and double_stretch_zebra. Frozen v11 comparisons
retain valid local orientations in those examples. The unconstrained learned
residual is a geometry correction and does not preserve FEM invertibility.
Large minimum-J magnitude depends on poor/skinny tetrahedra; negative orientation
still precludes calling the corrected mesh a valid elastic simulation.

All-finite predictions and improved marker errors do not override this finding.
Report both geometric scores and inversion counts. Do not silently repair or
replace the frozen primary after inspecting the regression cohort. A later
material-space, orientation-preserving correction is a separate future method.

Numerical qualification and separate prototypes
-----------------------------------------------
Primary reduced-contact dt refinements are not uniformly monotonic. The80-substep
setting was selected on development data; that is not proof of general timestep
convergence. Frozen shape and projection can hide dynamic deformation errors.

Independent coupled_qualification.py demonstrates a small spring lattice pushing
a movable sphere with equal/opposite reactions, friction, gravity and position
actuation. Its final stated conservation/refinement/penetration gates pass, and
the earlier failed low-damping diagnostic is preserved. This tests that separate
prototype only. It does not retroactively validate the external benchmark contact
model or infer material parameters from images.

A separate scene_fem diagnostic genuinely changes elastic shape on impact and
passes derivative/freefall tests, but documented contact/friction temporal
sensitivity remains. It is appropriately not promoted to primary. Keep these
three scopes distinct: benchmark reduced geometry predictor; synthetic coupled
mechanics qualification; development elastic scene-contact candidate.

Paper-safe conclusion
---------------------
Object-frame calibration and explicit static scene support improve geometry
prediction over the prior method on the established regression cohort. Strong
rigid and official PhysTwin baselines remain competitive or better, and corrected
geometry is not guaranteed physically admissible. General interaction inference,
full multi-body contact, calibrated uncertainty and reliable active probing are
not established by this campaign. Confirmation results should be reported as a
separate untouched evaluation, including failures and missing outputs.
