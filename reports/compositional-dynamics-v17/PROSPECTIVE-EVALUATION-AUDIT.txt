V17 PROSPECTIVE DYNAMIC EVALUATION — DRAFT, NOT A TEST FREEZE

Purpose
Test whether the existing frozen shared bulk/interface laws compose into useful dynamic predictions under load/unload, release, gravity, bouncing and impact. Compare physical and visual performance separately. Rendering parity or a beneficial tradeoff is a hypothesis, not an acceptance assumption. Preserve v16 code, weights and all static evidence.

Minimal staged experiment
1. Development only: free-flight/rigid invariance; an unconstrained elastic oscillation; prescribed compression followed by release; drop onto a plane. Establish consistent inertia, physical dissipation and contact/energy accounting before a corpus.
2. Four development worlds spanning the two trained constitutive families and two compliance regimes. Each receives three graded episodes below. Fix numerical settings, useful deformation range and camera specification using these worlds only.
3. Proposed held-out eight world specifications with fresh continuous material/support draws and geometric variation, all declared before generation. Each receives the same three episode families, giving24 full dynamic episodes. Group uncertainty intervals by world, not frames or camera views. Repeated geometries are not independent shape categories. The exact draw seeds, dimensions, strain/velocity ranges and testaction identities must be committed in the final protocol after development qualification.

Three essential dynamic episode families
A. Position-driven compress/hold/unload/release: about5%,15%,30% commanded compression are development grades. Save actual achieved strain and fixture slip; command magnitude is not deformation evidence. Primary held-out commands combine an unseen direction or release timing with the qualified range. This isolates material response and large deformation without collision confounding.
B. Released drop and multiple bounce on a plane: height set through impact energy relative to elastic energy, e.g rho*g*h/E, not height alone. Observe preimpact and at least two rebounds or a declared settling horizon. Report absence of rebound rather than excluding highly damped cases. No fitted restitution parameter supplied to only one method.
C. Oblique impact with initial rotation onto the same known plane, or a finite-radius smooth obstacle only if the plane study qualifies. Select one in development and freeze it; do not expand indefinitely. With friction absent, explicitly call this frictionless oblique normal impact; tangential sliding is not a friction benchmark.
Keep per-world low/medium/high grades for development diagnostics; choose the reserved physical range prospectively. If the highest grade fails, retain that exact development failure and declare a bounded valid range. Never remove a reserved case for solver failure or small deformation.

Information and baselines
First primary track is ORACLE physical descriptors: identical rest geometry, density, gravity, initial pose/velocity, tool trajectories, collision geometry and constitutive descriptors for all capable methods. The shared law uses frozen v16 weights; matched analytic teacher dynamics isolates learned-law error. Add coarse analytic dynamics and a rigid-body/contact baseline. Existing custom direct predictor is static and must not be presented as a dynamic forecasting baseline without separate dynamic training and matched label budgets.
A named existing framework comparison is required when its solver can execute these tasks: run its actual implementation with documented constitutive/contact capabilities and settings. Matching small-strain modulus is insufficient to call a nonlinear-family mismatch an equal-law solver comparison. Provide a shared-law-family/common-denominator track for solver comparison, and keep the two-family learned-law transfer track separate. A matched native kernel alone is not the complete authors' vision system. Do not claim framework inferiority from incompatible contact, mass, damping, or labels.
Only after the forward track works, use a separately frozen inference track with a common observed position prefix and known controls; no future geometry, force, contact state or true latent fields in fitting. Optional observed wrench is a separate track. Use the same prefix frames, sensor noise, compute/fit budget and calibration actions across methods. Oracle and inferred results must never be pooled.

Truth and numerical qualification
A fine simulation with the same energy/solver is a numerical reference, not independent physical ground truth. Both primary and companion resolution must pass timestep checks before using their spatial difference. Check contact quadrature/contact stiffness sensitivity separately; keeping the same penalty across meshes does not establish hard-contact accuracy. Use at least an analytic free-fall/oscillator control and one independent discretization/framework check in an equal-law regime. Report disagreement rather than adjudicating by whichever favors our model.
Proposed dimensionless development gates, to be finalized BEFORE any qualification output: full-trajectory world coordinate RMS<=0.005L; proper-rigid-aligned shape RMS<=0.0025L; contact impulse difference<=5% plus an explicitly fixed low-impulse floor; bounce apex difference<=0.02L and event-time difference<=0.01sqrt(L/g). L is the smallest undeformed object dimension, fixed perworld. These are suggested task accuracy targets, not retrospective permissions to relax a failed gate. Physics agent must confirm an affordable pair or document a different justified target prospectively.
All states finite, positive physical mass, valid J, no hidden rest-position pinning in free dynamics. Work-energy ledger must include gravity potential, strain energy, kinetic energy with actual mass operator, contact penalty storage, actuator work and dissipated work. In zero-dissipation/no-actuator controls, measure timestep-dependent energy drift; in dissipative scenes, compare balance residual, not raw energy conservation. Predeclare admissible penetration relative to L; do not call nonzero penalty penetration exact nonpenetration. Record unsampled/missed collisions and tunnelling. End a failed trajectory as failed, never silently clamp or restart it with softer parameters.

Physics metrics, all episodes and failures
World position RMSE; mass-weighted COM and orientation error; best-proper-rigid-removed deformation RMS; worldmotion/deformation ratio across every episode; principal strain and minimumJ; tool/collider force and torque plus integrated impulse; penetration/contact timing; impact speed, rebound apex and effective restitution; oscillation frequency/damping; work-energy residual. Report absolute and dimensionless units, complete-frame coverage, first failure time and solver retries. Compare peak-force traces only at compatible sample rates; impulse is less sampling-sensitive. Coverage-conditional means must show their denominator alongside fixed cohort counts.

Visual metrics and videos
Same solved state feeds every rendered view, with fixed known appearance, calibrated cameras, identical lighting and pixel geometry. No independent image warp, motion scaling, per-method camera fitting or truth-based alignment. Report RGB errors over full image and foreground union, depth error on shared valid pixels together with missing/extra-depth rates, and silhouette IoU. Do not hide missing shape behind an intersection-only depth metric. Render metric tables separately from physical metrics; diagnose whether visual agreement masks mechanical disagreement.
For each episode family predeclare one representative world and one failure/worst-case selection rule. Produce actual-time load/unload/drop/bounce trajectories with synchronized analytic-reference/prediction panels, timestamps and physical scale. Include a fixed camera as the primary motion view and an additional modest common orbit. Never replace dynamics with a static camera orbit or interpolate equilibria and call it a rollout. Show real simulation sampling and any slow-motion factor; no exaggerated deformation.

Essential launch gates
G1: operator/mass/contact sign and energy control checks pass; source/weights and v16 preservation manifest pinned.
G2: development timestep/spatial/contact consistency and deformation census completed, including failed grades; external-framework capability/common-input table reviewed.
G3: exact held-out cohort/action/camera/metrics/failure policy and all method settings frozen before targets are generated; predictions committed before scoring access.
G4: final all24 accounting, independent raw-metric check and actual dynamic video QA. Positive outcome not required; integrity pass does not mean better dynamics.

Current implementation hazards from repository inspection
v13 original node-contact symplectic/lumped model explicitly lacks CCD/self-contact and previously failed sharp-drop spatial tests. Its later repaired surface/contact work is reusable evidence, not automatic qualification for impacts or a new constitutive law. external_benchmark/dynamic_fem.py explicitly removes a static rest-position gauge under inertia; this must remain absent in release dynamics. v16 custom DeepSets forecasts equilibria, not trajectories. v16 normal-contact transfer is quasistatic and uses a fixed numerical penalty; it cannot validate bounce, momentum exchange, or physical stiffness identification by itself.

ADDITIONAL RENDERING CONTRACT (before any v17 evaluation)
There are TWO distinct tracks. The common known-appearance renderer isolates errors in physical state; using that backend for both methods cannot establish native-framework rendering parity. A separate native-appearance/Gaussian track must fit appearance from the SAME observed RGB prefix, camera calibration and initial geometry budget. Held future frames and views stay unavailable during appearance fitting. Our material-attached Gaussians may reuse the same Gaussian backend but move only through the simulated material field; no independent learned motion correction. Neither method receives privileged truth textures or extra training images. Record appearance parameters, fitting iterations/data and wallclock budget. Report PSNR/SSIM and an explicitly named perceptual metric when implemented, plus depth/mask metrics with missing-pixel accounting. Native framework quality, common-backend quality and physical accuracy are separate tables, not interchangeable evidence.

INITIAL OPERATOR REVIEW
The new v17 DynamicContactModel/VelocityVerlet uses the inherited energy residual, sparse consistent Q2 mass, two-sided sphere forces and gravitational potential. The free support mode zeroes bilateral matrices and targets, avoiding an artificial restoring spring. Spherical rotation needs no orientation state for isotropic normal sphere geometry; that does not generalize to boxes or frictional contact. The four proposed tests appropriately check mass moments/ballistics, second-order manufactured oscillation, coupled momentum/objectivity, and independent stable-NH energy/stress. Source review permits these small operator tests. It does not authorize corpus generation or qualify large impacts. Pilot launch remains contingent on exact source/plan pins, serializer and accounting review.

FAMILY C DECISION (before held-out generation)
Family C is a finite-mass rigid sphere striker impacting a bilaterally supported soft block. It explicitly exercises two-way impulse and the shared bulk plus attachment laws. It is not replaceable by a second oblique drop without declaring the coupled-impact objective unfinished. Oblique drop may instead be an OOD variation of family B. The bounded proposal remains eight held-out worlds times three episode families (24 episodes), after four development worlds and numerical qualification. No hidden removal of impact when it is difficult.
