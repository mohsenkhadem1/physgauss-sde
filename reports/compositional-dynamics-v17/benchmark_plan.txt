V17 FRAMEWORK COMPARISON PLAN — prospective adapter design, not a frozen experiment

Objective and minimum comparison
Use true time-dependent drops, rebounds, finite-mass impacts, and large prescribed
bending/compression/twist. Retain initial position/velocity and complete time series;
a camera orbit of settled states is not dynamic evidence. Compare physical prediction
and image quality in separate tables. Ours is not assumed to win before evaluation.

1. Executable existing framework: stock Newton VBD1.7.0 / Warp1.18.0
Isolated installed runtime: runs/operator-dev/newton-runtime-v01/env.
Unmodified SolverVBD supports tetrahedral soft bodies and coupled rigid bodies.
Installed material kernel is stable Neo-Hookean, with mu_NH=mu_Lame and
lambda_NH=lambda_Lame+mu_Lame; alpha=1+mu_NH/lambda_NH. This finite-strain energy
is not the v16 Mooney-Rivlin/Yeoh family. Native smoke passed: CPU,4particles,
1tet,8x1ms freefall steps, minJ .999999747, finite trajectory; API feasibility only.
Do not reuse historical accepted-iterate/BDF2/momentum/area-contact wrappers as
stock Newton. Those are explicitly modified numerical/control formulations.

Mandatory constitutive tiers:
 A. Solver-control: exact stable-NH energy in both native VBD and our analytical
    dynamics, identical geometry/mass/gravity/commands/initial velocity and declared
    contact model as closely supported. Diagnose mesh/time/iteration/contact-law
    differences separately. This is not a learned-law accuracy comparison.
 B. Model-family transfer: same MR/Yeoh teacher cases and observed calibration data;
    native stable-NH is explicitly a restricted constitutive family, fitted fairly
    to the same calibration commands. Shared laws and exact-family analytic ceiling
    are separate columns. Do not call the family mismatch an engine defect.

Physical damping and numerical dissipation:
Declare physical viscosity/dashpot/damping and contact restitution separately from
integration. VBD implicit integration can dissipate impact energy numerically;
Verlet conserving more energy is not by itself more physically accurate. Refine
all methods' dt/iterations on development until rebound height, impulse and field
errors stabilize. Include zero-physical-damping controls and a separately declared
nonzero-damping case. Keep native solver limitations/failures with denominators.

Information and rendering controls
Oracle track: same known geometry, initial state, density/gravity, constitutive
information supported by each method and prescribed tool pose trajectories; no
future response labels. Unknown-parameter track: identical observed prefix frames,
visibility masks/noise, calibration command budget and optional force track.
Provide measured forces to every method in that track or to none. Exclude hidden
teacher labels from inference. Author methods may fit more parameters but their
training-data and optimization budgets must be recorded; do not deliberately
undertrain them. Freeze after development and retain every held-out failure.

Physics-controlled image comparison: all predicted states pass through the same
calibrated renderer, cameras, surface samples, material-attached texture, lighting
and visibility rules. No state-dependent image warp. This isolates geometry errors;
it does not compare native rendering quality or demonstrate novel view synthesis.
Native-rendering comparison: separate native PhysTwin GS and Isaac RTX outputs,
with identical train-view/prefix image budget and held cameras/times. Report
appearance training, surface/Gaussian binding, exposure/resolution and compute.
If ours shares the same fitted Gaussian appearance/binding, that is a controlled
same-appearance deformation test, not an independent renderer superiority claim.
No unobserved ground-truth texture/geometry advantage without explicit oracle label.

Metrics and visible success criteria
Physics: world-position and rigid-aligned shape errors, velocities, timing of
first impact/separation, rebound height, contact impulse and momentum balance,
energy/work/dissipation balance, stress/strain when reference-qualified, minimumJ,
penetration and contact failures. Force peaks need matched temporal sampling;
impulse is more robust. Report deformation magnitude to reject rigid-motion-only
success. Renderer: foreground-union RGB error, PSNR/SSIM as appropriate, joint-
visible depth error, silhouetteIoU, missing/extra pixels; LPIPS only with pinned
model. Both static heldviews and dynamic heldtimes, same masks acrossmethods.
Define rendering equivalence/noninferiority margins prospectively on development;
'no significant difference' alone does not prove rendering parity. Pair perobject
and scenario, preserve failures, report uncertainty with objects as clusters.

Prototype-specific comparability note (before new dynamic comparison)
The public prototype is a .12 by .06 by .06 m block, density625 kg/m3,
E12000 Pa, nu.35, free fall20mm onto planez=-.05m. Its conservative
normal pressure law is4e7 N/m3 integrated over reference surface area.
Stock Newton soft_contact_ke is a global particle/shape stiffness, not a
per-node reference-area field. The identical numeric value is NOT a valid
conversion. Any native drop comparison must record its vertex-contact law,
particle radius/offset and area-normalization approximation explicitly,
qualify mesh dependence, and distinguish contact-model error from integrator
error. Native lumped tet mass versus consistent Q2 mass is another explicit
discretization difference. Exact bulk-energy matching alone does not imply
identical spatial or contact operators.

Native PhysTwin appearance adapter input contract (source inspected)
QQTT reader expects camera_meta.pkl intrinsics+c2ws, fixed848x480 RGB{i}.png,
optional mask_{i}.png and {i}_depth.npy IN MILLIMETRES (converted to metres),
observation.ply and/or shape_prior.glb; interp_poses.pkl defines render-only
held cameras. All camera_meta cameras are marked training, so supply ONLY
allowed training cameras there. Its interpolated test cameras have image=None;
keep withheld images exclusively in our evaluator, not in the authortraining
folder. Reader chooses test intrinsics from training camera1, so use common
calibration or explicitly record an adapter rather than silently changing it.
Known canonical initialgeometry is available equally to all methods in this
track. Native GS scene training does not establish inverse dynamics accuracy;
it is a separate appearance stage, with allowed prefix only for mechanical fit.

REQUIRED inference comparison before any claim over PhysTwin
Oracle known-descriptor Newton/shared-law forward controls establish only
forward validity. To claim better physics than a vision-based framework, run
full native PhysTwin and our inferred model with the SAME observed calibration
prefix: position-commanded compression/indentation before held drop/impact.
Freeflight alone is not an informative elasticity experiment. Match initial
geometry knowledge, image/depth/position visibility, timestamps and command
tracks; both estimate unknown physical parameters from that prefix. Neither
receives future forces, future shape or true material/support descriptors.
Declare optimizer budgets, priors and native model restrictions before tests.
A claim of better physics than full PhysTwin is unavailable if this stage
cannot execute; the oracle solver/control study is not a substitute. Native
appearance parity likewise uses the same allowed appearance observations and
held evaluation cameras. This is a required comparison stage, not an optional
appendix to an oracle-versus-inferred comparison.

Native inverse data adapter outline
RealData consumes object_points[T,N,3], object_colors, object_visibilities,
object_motions_valid, controller_points, fixedsurface_points/interior_points.
Its constructor loads all supplied frames and bydefault saves a GTvideo.
Therefore export prefix-only tracked objectpoints for fitting, plus public
initialsurface/interior geometry (same to both methods). For forecast, extend
only prescribed controllercommands and use explicitly invalid object-target
placeholders; never hand nativeRealData the evaluator's future objecttracks.
Disable display/GTvideo sideeffects in a declared headless wrapper. Verify
future-target mutation invariance across export, fit and forecast. This retains
the author's graph/per-spring fitting/integration semantics while separating
input preparation from evaluation. No full inverse benchmark has run yet.
