V17 mechanics feasibility and initial implementation

Choice: retain the qualified v16 Q2 energy and normal-contact operators, and
compose them with a new, separately tested consistent Q2 inertia and velocity-
Verlet timestep wrapper. This preserves the frozen learned bulk/interface law
API. It does not interpolate equilibria or use a renderer to invent motion.

Reuse inspected:
- v16 ContactQ2Mechanics provides actual-surface quadrature, conservative plane/
  sphere penalty energies, equal/opposite force and torque accounting, positive-J
  guards, and explicit gravity. Its static qualification is not dynamic evidence.
- v13 consistent mass and momentum tests establish the correct Mv bookkeeping;
  v17 assembles rho integral(Ni Nj) in the actual Q2 mapped reference geometry,
  rather than copying P1/lumped masses or tuning a nodal inertia correction.
- Older nonlinear dynamics has tested implicit backward-Euler mechanics and
  finite rigid handles, but its constitutive/discretization assumptions differ.
- Native Newton VBD uses stable Neo-Hookean and backward Euler; old drop results
  were not universally timestep/spatially consistent. The framework lane owns
  stock Newton reproduction. A new exact stable-NH common-law energy is checked
  against the independent existing NumPy law before comparison. MR/Yeoh learned
  transfer remains a separate constitutive experiment.

Implementation: scripts/compositional_dynamics_v17/dynamics.py.
DynamicContactModel supports exact free release or bilateral attachments.
VelocityVerlet uses a once-factored consistent mass, kicks/drifts physical nodal
positions and velocities, and evolves finite-mass sphere translation/angular
velocity with matching reaction forces. Sphere orientation is presently omitted
because normal contact with a uniform sphere is orientation-independent; no
oriented rigid-body or frictional-spin claim. Sphere/plane rigid-rigid contact
outside the soft interaction is rejected rather than silently ignored.

Current independent operator evidence: four tests passed in2.45s: exact uniform
mass/second moments and ballistic gravity; manufactured harmonic convergence and
energy; coupled sphere momentum/angular momentum and rigid objectivity; exact
stable-NH energy/stress agreement. These are operator checks, not a drop benchmark.

Prospective bounded initial rollout: two development cases,8 mesh/timestep jobs,
CPU1/1200seconds, source-pinned plan and all failures/missing identities retained.
Free20% compression release and20mm floor drop use conservative material/contact
energy, zero physical damping and no restitution reset. Save actual1ms physical
samples, full per-step energy/force/momentum/J, same-state surface fields and
independent dense sampled gaps. Larger deformation and impact require new graded
qualification after this feasibility check; no expensive training authorized here.

Outstanding scientific checks: elastic impact timestep/mesh/contact quadrature
convergence; energy including penalty storage and any later actuator/damping
work; sufficiently large measured nonrigid deformation; meaningful bounce
apex/timing/impulse and possible tunneling; learned-law strain-range validity;
matched physical inputs with stock framework baselines. Finite penalty is not
exact nonpenetration. Conservative bounce is not physical viscoelastic damping.
Explicit Verlet has a stability limit; failure will not trigger silent timestep,
material, mass, damping, contact or restitution changes.
