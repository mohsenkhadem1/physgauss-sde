OVERALL DECISION
The controlled-contact/drop teaser is substantially stronger than the SoftVT cube/slip teaser. It visibly communicates changed interaction and deformation, includes genuine reference-matched zooms, and uses a common physical error scale. Preserve it as the starting point, but integrate real-data breadth without implying that within-sequence PhysTwin reconstruction is a win: the existing main real comparison loses to official PhysTwin. The stronger real contribution is frozen cross-interaction transfer on two qualifying pairs. Full cohorts, analytic control and failures must remain visible elsewhere.
