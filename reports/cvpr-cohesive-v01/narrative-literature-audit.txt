RECOMMENDED CENTER
Title candidate: Compositional Deformable Twins: Predicting Physical Responses Beyond Visual Agreement.
One-line thesis: fit effective material and interaction descriptors from observed positions, retain those descriptors when querying changed interactions, and evaluate deformation/reactions separately from rendered agreement.
This is one representation principle instantiated by distinct numerical adapters. It is NOT one universal checkpoint, a new general contact engine, or an end-to-end raw-video model.

DEFENSIBLE CONTRIBUTIONS
1. A concrete compositional inverse-mechanics model combining a compact invariant scalar bulk energy, separately localized compliant attachments, explicit environmental loads/contact and Gaussian appearance transport. State clearly which parts are learned and which are analytic in each adapter.
2. An observability argument and measured counterexample explaining why equal images need not imply equal reactions; an additional-observation experiment showing improved held-response point predictions. The scaling argument is a physical symmetry, not a new general identifiability theorem.
3. A matched-input evaluation across controlled changed interactions, prospective simulated manipulation episodes and limited real cross-interaction transfer, reporting motion, rigid-aligned deformation, rendering and coverage separately.

UNSUPPORTED CLAIMS TO REMOVE OR AVOID
- First separation of physics and appearance: Spring-Gaus and PhysTwin already separate these stages.
- First physical twin with future or novel-interaction prediction: both already demonstrate these.
- First shared or reusable constitutive law: NCLaw and especially UniPhy precede it.
- First constitutive Gaussian field: PIDG and OmniPhysGS are direct prior work.
- Infer arbitrary boundary conditions or contacts from video: current studies prescribe interaction structure and supplied tracks/geometry; fitting footprint/strength is narrower.
- Absolute real stiffness/force recovery: unknown prestress, geometry and force/mass scale prevent that conclusion.
- Gaussian energy footprint automatically models all force, contact, adhesion and friction laws: bilateral attachment differs from unilateral contact, dissipation and body force.
- SOTA on native SoftVT task: current protocol is privileged trajectory forecasting, not native closed-loop manipulation.
- Rendering superiority from shared-appearance PSNR: common-renderer comparisons measure trajectory/transport consequences, not independently fitted native rendering quality.
- Flow's latest validation as the primary method: strongest controls beat it; keep as diagnostic and preserve prior best.

PRIMARY LITERATURE VERIFIED ONLINE
PhysTwin (ICCV2025), https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.17973 and https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/papers/Jiang_PhysTwin_Physics-Informed_Reconstruction_and_Simulation_of_Deformable_Objects_from_Videos_ICCV_2025_paper.pdf : sparse-viewRGB-D, geometry completion, spring-mass inverse modeling and Gaussian appearance; evaluates reconstruction, future prediction and unseen interactions. A claim that it only reconstructs observed motion is false. Existing related-work description is substantively fair.
Spring-Gaus (ECCV2024), https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/00271.pdf : integrates spring-mass dynamics with Gaussian rendering from multiview video; explicitly separates appearance/geometry and physical reconstruction and tests changed initial/environmental conditions. Compare configured adaptations and acknowledge its own inference task.
UniPhy (CVPR2025), https://arxiv.org/html/2505.16971v1 and https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/papers/Mittal_UniPhy_Learning_a_Unified_Constitutive_Model_for_Inverse_Physics_Simulation_CVPR_2025_paper.pdf : shared latent-conditioned projection/stress networks, frozen weights and latent optimization from particle trajectories; elastic/plastic/sand/fluid coverage and new configurations. Our scalar-energy composition is narrower, not first reuse. One completed native query is insufficient to rank its method globally.
NCLaw (ICML2023), https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/ma23a.html : embeds structured constitutive networks in differentiable governing equations and tests changed geometry, initial/boundary conditions and time ranges. Reusable material response is established prior art.
PIDG (AAAI2026 entry already in bibliography), https://arxiv.org/html/2511.06299v1 : monocular dynamic novel-view reconstruction using time-dependent Gaussian deformation, velocity/stress, momentum residuals and camera-compensated optical flow. Its stated problem is spatiotemporal reconstruction; the reviewed formulation does not expose a commanded-force/position reset simulator interface. Distinguish tasks without alleging it cannot ever predict or that ours empirically beats it. No numerical PIDG comparison exists here. Publisher URL returned bot denial today; arXiv primary text was available and verified.
PhysWorld (2025preprint), https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.21447 : physics-aware synthesized demonstrations train efficient deformable world models. Relevant when discussing simulation-trained predictors and action-conditioned generalization; optional concise addition to related work, no unimplemented baseline claim. Bib addition supplied separately.

Source details that prevent accidental overstatement:
- v18material_scene_fit names are scale,log_bulk,contrast,center,+second_scale; shape=0,family_flag=-1 are explicitly inserted. The spatial material field is scale+bodyoffset+contrast*tanh(8*(X1-center)); it is not six Gaussian material kernels as in realtetrahedral.
- v18SceneBody inherits DynamicContactModel; it does not learn arbitrary topology. Geometry is a fixed mappedblock in the main24world study; geometry transfer appears only in the older separate oracle study.
- v18RigidBowl.signed_distance is a normalized implicit gap, not an exact Euclidean signed distance. Use 'gap function' for the generic penalty equation; no universal signed-distance claim.
- v18CoupledScene.controls requires translation-only prescribed actuator poses. Generic pose notation is fine for the broad representation, but experiments should not claim tested arbitrary fixture rotation in this six-familyadapter.
- RealNeuralFEM divides pretrained energy by12000 for normalized units and replaces analyticbulk only; inherited explicit attachments and floor are unchanged. AbsoluteSIstiffness identifiability is not established.
- RealExternalFEM includes numerical pin1e-5 to initialgeometry. Avoid claiming every external object is a perfectly free deformable body or no artificial support exists. Supplement should disclose it as weak numerical regularization.
