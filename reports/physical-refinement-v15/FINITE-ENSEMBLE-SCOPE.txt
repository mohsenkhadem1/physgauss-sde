Pre-reserved-evaluation scope decision

The final declared gradient refinement (9 to 17 nodes) failed the unchanged
posterior convergence criteria. The 49 to 97 common-scale integration passed
all 704 fixed development histories. No additional gradient levels will be
added, and no observations/noise will be changed to force a pass.

The original deterministic raster observation operator is retained. The bounded
2x/4x pixel-footprint diagnostic reduced one extreme cross-mesh discrepancy,
but did not establish fixed-state rendered-feature convergence. It is not an
accepted numerical remedy. This remains a synthetic image-feature experiment
with material numerical observation mismatch, not a validated physical camera
model. Independent off-grid truth worlds will still use finer mechanical meshes.

We will evaluate the existing 17-gradient/97-scale finite physical-belief model
as an exploratory predictor and acquisition heuristic. Its predictions can be
useful or fail; only measured independent response error establishes which.
We cannot call this a converged continuous reference posterior, claim calibrated
parameter uncertainty, or infer physical accuracy from posterior narrowing.
Empirical predictive coverage, proper scores, and predicted-versus-realized risk
remain measured outcomes. Failed refinement tables remain part of the results.

The controlled rendering uses known geometry and appearance with actual RGB-D
visibility; it is not a learned Gaussian scene reconstruction. This corrects
the intended renderer-reuse wording in the earlier development-plan.json.
The reaction-augmented track includes both force and torque, reported separately.

No reserved truths or responses have been generated or inspected at this
decision. Strong fixed schedules will be chosen only from development, then
frozen along with policies, actions, likelihood, sources and seeds. Continuous
truth parameters will not be snapped to ensemble nodes. The original benchmark
results remain unchanged. This study cannot establish improvements on those
benchmarks; it targets the separate visual-fit/physical-accuracy claim.
