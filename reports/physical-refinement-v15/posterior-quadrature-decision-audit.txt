Prospective bounded posterior-resolution rule (before comparing results)
The initial 60 categorical hypotheses remain a coarse ablation, not the primary continuous-scale posterior. The earlier interval-collapse issue makes a numerical posterior check central to this study.

Scale integration
Choose and freeze a continuous prior, recommended log-uniform s∈[.75,1.5], independent of the declared discrete support/fixture-ratio and gradient prior. Use Gauss–Legendre integration over log(s): transformed nodes s=exp(z), positive quadrature weights including the prior density/Jacobian exactly. Do not assign equal probability to Gauss nodes. This is25 then49 nodes per20 geometric hypotheses (500 then980 particles), with no additional FEM because common E/K scaling leaves geometry unchanged and scales reactions. Validate that mechanics invariance for the adopted extremes/actions before exploiting it. Preserve evidence normalization and prior weights; an unnormalized discrete likelihood sum is not a continuous evidence estimate.
A bounded fallback is97 nodes, only if25/49 fails. Accept49 if25/49 meets every declared criterion; otherwise require49/97 and accept97 if it passes. No further ladder or tolerance relaxation within this primary study. Failure means the posterior is not numerically qualified, not that a sharper interval is preferable.

Development inputs and checks
Use only four development worlds, middle sensor noise and fixed predeclared measurement seeds. Check D0, every available first-query observation and all unordered two-query combinations under the independent-reset/common-noise-packet contract. These are development observations, not final test responses. Full posterior summaries need no action-scoring Monte Carlo. Acquisition-policy score convergence needs checking at D0 and each single-observation history only, since budget two never chooses a third query. This keeps the diagnostic finite. Use the same design-query bank and declared risk feature scales at every quadrature level.
Record log evidence, posterior scale mean/std/quantiles, predictive means/std and selected-query scores. Posterior quantiles must be computed from weighted mass, not interpolated as an unweighted sample. Describe quadrature-approximated distribution quantiles honestly; predictive coverage is a separate empirical evaluation.
Suggested fixed tolerances:
- maximum development-history predictive coordinate-mean difference across design queries: <=0.05 mm coordinate RMS;
- coordinate predictive standard-deviation difference: <=0.01 mm +5% of finer-level standard deviation, componentwise or a predeclared query RMS aggregation (freeze which);
- force mean difference: <=1e-4 N +0.5% of finer-level force magnitude; force standard-deviation difference <=1e-4 N +5%;
- torque analogues use1e-5 Nm absolute floor;
- absolute log-evidence difference <=0.01;
- continuous scale mean difference <=0.002; std difference <=0.002+5%; 5th/95th quantile differences <=0.01 in scale units. The quantile check prevents apparent interval stability being inferred from means alone.
The tolerances are proposals to be adopted explicitly before comparisons. The error budget must remain smaller than the physical change the paper interprets. Record every failure instead of changing a criterion to obtain approval.

Policy-score comparison
Use common random uniforms/standard normals for fantasies across resolutions so differences are interpretable. Equal RNG seed alone does not guarantee identical latent parameter draws when quadrature nodes differ; inverse-CDF coupling using common uniforms gives a useful coupling. Retain per-query score SE and actual pairwise score-difference SE where possible. Use a development diagnostic count fixed in advance (suggest512 fantasies); use1024 only as a predeclared MC precision fallback. Scores whose numerical change is indistinguishable from Monte Carlo error should not trigger endless grid refinement. Conversely a clearly separated ranking reversal is a failure.
A practical rule is require same maximizing action OR the competing actions' score gap is <=2 combined Monte Carlo SE at both levels. Also require per-query score differences within max(2 combined SE, 1% of initial design risk). Report unresolved ties and stable declaration-order tie breaking. This qualifies numerical selection stability, not actual acquisition superiority.

Gradient integration/resolution
Five gradients {-0.8,-0.4,0,.4,.8} can initially be a declared discrete model, but continuous off-grid truths make its mismatch visible. For a continuous-gradient reference posterior, compare5 versus9 equally spaced nodes using explicit trapezoidal prior weights on[-.8,.8]; endpoints carry half weight. This adds20 geometric cases (2 layouts×4 new gradients×2 ratios), not a new solver. Apply the same predictive/evidence/scale-summary criteria at the accepted scale quadrature level. If5/9 fails, do not call the posterior converged. Either predeclare one bounded17-node fallback before inspecting9-node outcomes or retain9 as a declared approximate model with unresolved discretization, without a trustworthy-reference-posterior claim. Do not conflate a finite hypothesis model with numerical quadrature of a continuous prior.
Known discrete fixture-ratio classes and support alternatives remain discrete in both models. A future continuous-ratio experiment requires its own integration check; it is not covered here.

Discrepancy and evidence boundaries
Hold sensor/model discrepancy fixed while checking quadrature. Inflating likelihood noise until a coarse grid passes is invalid. Separately freeze numerical/model discrepancy from matched-parameter development coarse/reference differences. No reserved predictive outcomes or coverage are used for mesh, quadrature, fantasy count, noise, prior or tolerance selection. Passing these checks establishes numerical consistency of this small assumed posterior; misspecified physics or likelihood can still yield confident wrong predictions.
