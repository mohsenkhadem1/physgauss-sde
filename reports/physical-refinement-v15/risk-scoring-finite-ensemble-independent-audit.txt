Risk algebra
The new acquisition implementation is algebraically equivalent to the former particle-Gram calculation. For centered target vectors z_h and posterior weights p_h, expected coordinate-averaged squared loss is Σ_h p_h mean(z_h²)−mean((Σ_h p_h z_h)²). The direct moment implementation computes exactly this quantity, avoiding the H×H Gram matrix. Centering target and whitened sensor means changes neither posterior residual distances nor variance. Fantasies use the same discrete ancestor sampling and normal noise; the provided comparison checks risk reduction, Monte Carlo SE and effective sample size against the original implementation. This is a computational rearrangement, not a new acquisition objective.
The whitened squared-distance expansion is subject to floating-point cancellation for extremely large feature magnitudes; centering reduces common-offset issues. Current evidence does not claim arbitrary dynamic-range robustness. Negative tiny squared distances/variances are clipped to zero. Predicted risk is conditional on the finite ensemble and assumed likelihood, not a guarantee about the continuous physical world.
Additional prefreeze contract hardening: check that target leading dimension equals hypothesis count before reshape, so a wrongly shaped array with the same total size cannot silently be reinterpreted. Actual bank manifests must independently bind that hypothesis ordering.

Predictive scoring
For scalar coordinates, weighted CRPS is E|X−y|−0.5E|X−X'|. Sorting gives the implemented half-pairwise term Σ_i w_i x_i(2 cumulative_i−w_i−1) when weights sum EXACTLY to one. The fixture verifies this identity against explicit pairwise distances and the point-mass absolute-error limit. Mean marginal CRPS is a proper score for coordinate marginals, not a strictly proper score of the full joint response distribution or covariance. Coordinate90% coverage is a descriptive fraction over correlated response coordinates, not a90% simultaneous shape guarantee. Keep coordinate, force and torque scores in separate units or use an explicitly fixed normalization; never average m,N,Nm without definition.
Confirmed bug reported to root: the current np.isclose(sum(weights),1) admits1.000001. With a single prediction=target=1000 this returns negative CRPS−.001000001 because the formula assumes exact normalization. Normalize finite nonnegative one-dimensional weights after validation, or reject with an exact suitable contract. Reject empty target coordinates and mismatched first dimensions. Do not hide the issue by simply clipping negative CRPS. No claim that production weights currently exhibit this deviation; this is an independently reproduced robustness defect.
