OBSERVATION DESIGN — development proposal, not a frozen evaluation

Reuse/API
- scripts/phystwin/v06_camera.py implements perspective-correct triangle z-buffer RGB-D plus explicitly supplied colored fiducials on fixed81-node mesh. Its z-buffer mathematics is reused in physical_refinement_v15.observations.render_scene; marker-ID painting is not reused.
- Camera(eye,target,width,height,focal) and render_scene(meshes,camera) return RGB, metric optical-axis depth and known camera calibration only. Meshes are actual deformed material surfaces and physical fixture/tool surfaces. No support/material IDs, solver state or observation-derived labels are returned.
- box_mesh is a pilot geometry utility, not a fixture label. Generic mesh inputs support fixed boundary-grid triangles displaced by SmoothMaterialFixture.query_field(u,rest_boundary). The exact same x(state,hypothesis,position-command) must feed both mechanics metrics and rendering; no image-specific corrected trajectory.
- src/physgauss/render.py already supplies material-barycentric Gaussians with covariance F Sigma0 F^T. It is RGB-only, hardcodes sigma0=.105m and focal=size*1.65; continuous_render assumes near/far1.3–3.8m. Reusing it requires declared world-scale covariance/camera/depth adaptation, not blindly applying demo defaults to24cm objects. A Q2-to-sampled-tet render bridge can query identical mechanical states. Mesh RGBD is the immediate sensor; do not mislabel it Gaussian reconstruction or a learned renderer.

Minimal observation pilot
An opaque8x6x5cm block with an actual gray12x20x16mm rearfixture at x=-20mm or+20mm. Frontcamera sees identical RGBD (RGBRMSE0/depth0); rearcamera seesRGBRMSE.02116/depth6.69mm. Support patches are at actual touching faces y=.03m, but this toy geometry has NOT been bound to the qualified mechanical model. Hiddenmaterial changes with identical exterior state/appearance produce exactly identical static views. This is a sensor sanity check, not physical identification evidence.

Actual-model effective fixture binding implemented (development only)
Physics proposes24x8x8cm block and Gaussian*taper endface attachment footprints. A small hardpatch mesh would not match a footprint nonzero over the entireface. The shared fixture_geometry generator now emits fixed-length octagonal ligaments with cross-section areas proportional to the exact footprint() sampled at declared cell centers. Its normalized areas match the effective Robin prior; correctedv2polygonradiiare calibrated to actual octagonal area, not circular nominal area. No arbitrary colored support labels. Camera may reveal support location/profile only; support stiffness K and hidden material heterogeneity must remain absent from appearance unless they cause observed motion. This is a visible graded effective attachment-density prior, not resolved isotropic rod elasticity or validated fixture mechanics. Inner endpoints follow the sameQ2materialstate and outer endpoints the same commandedpose. The known geometry and fixed appearance are supplied observation baselines, not learned Gaussian reconstruction.

Fair acquisition contract proposal
Every policy receives the same initial calibratedRGBD images and known position-command history. A view query returns actual renderedRGBD at a predeclared pose of the unchanged mechanical state. A poke query executes a prescribed position-controlled trajectory and returns RGBD at the same fixed3 physical timestamps for every policy. Match frame count/resolution/noise/number of acquisitions within each budget arm; a static view arm also returns3 frames (independent sensor noise), not a free dense point cloud. Report separate view/probe costs rather than assume camera movement equals interaction cost. Reaction force, if acquired, is a separate modality/budget arm, not quietly appended to images.

Inference/update likelihood must compare predicted renderedRGBD to acquiredRGBD. No hidden support/material lookup, truth-state nearest-node tracking, or hypothesis-index sensor. Camera calibration is public. Depth silhouette mismatch must be counted, not scored only on conveniently overlappingpixels. Invalid images/failed mechanically qualified responses remain failures. Mechanical actions reserved for testing may never drive ambiguity search, acquisition selection or camera placement; development hypotheses/actions can set choices before freeze.

Necessary counterexample
Two hypotheses with same unloaded geometry/appearance and different hidden heterogeneity must have identical static views. A new view cannot reduce their material ambiguity without motion or another mechanism that exposes it. Conversely a genuinely occluded support fixture can be revealed by an additional view. Claims must distinguish these regimes. Only measured new response, never confidence shrinkage alone, can establish improved physical prediction.

Feature sensor API (development extension)
physical_refinement_v15.sensor.SensorSettings exposes positive depth/RGB/force scales and fixed4x4tilemeans/farclip1m/64pxspatialcorrelation/0.5pxregistration covariance approximation. distribution(image, settings, force=None, visual_covariance=C) returns predictedmean, fullcovariance and explicitvisual/forceindices. measure(image,rng,...) returns only noisyfeaturevalues/covariance/indices/camera; no noiselessmean or hiddenmechanical labels. Optional force is its own independent Gaussian block (default0.01N), separately disclosed modality.

Crucial distinction: actual renderedRGBD is projected to fixed features first; then a multivariate Gaussian measurement is sampled. This is feature-level synthetic sensor noise, NOT pixel-level depth noise or randomly warped images. Registration Jacobians approximate correlated feature errors; they do not claim a calibrated physical camera. Clean RGBD images belong to internal prediction/diagnostic archives, not additional noise-free inference inputs. A protocol claiming raw RGBD availability must evaluate the raw modality separately.

Use a common fixed covariance per query, constructed from a public nominal reference image before hidden truth selection, for every candidate prediction and the measurement. Do not silently pool candidatecovariances based on unknown truths in deployed belief updates. The pairwise visibility diagnostic used pooled covariance explicitly for a sensitivity calculation; it is not yet a frozen operational sensor. Static equal images remain exactly equal distributions. Noise scales/correlation settings must be declared before reserved outcomes; no tuning to manufacture ambiguity.

Wrench extension before freeze
Sensor reaction argument accepts only shape(3,) force or shape(6,) [Fx,Fy,Fz,Tx,Ty,Tz] world-frame wrench. Torque requires explicit wrench_origin_world_m (planned right-tool center); no origin is guessed. Force_sd_N=.01 and torque_sd_Nm=.001 are distinct prescribed synthetic scales. force_indices, torque_indices, reaction_indices and channel_units distinguishN fromNm. A2x3 array of twoforces is rejected, not silently treated as a wrench. Existingpilotdata are unchanged.

The final production acquisition proposal now uses ONE settled RGBDfeature frame perquery forboth passiveview andposition-controlled probe, with each quasistaticprobe reset todeclaredinitialstate. This supersedes the earlier tentative3framebudget above; finalfrozenprotocol will specify exact modalities andquerycosts. Force/wrench remains an explicitly separate budget/modality arm.
