These videos compare actual simulated geometry with the initial and after-two-query highest-weight physical hypotheses. They show four independently settled held response states, with a modest common camera orbit and hard cuts between states. They do not show a dynamical rollout or interpolate motion between equilibria.

Colors are a fixed material-coordinate appearance pattern shared by all hypotheses. They do not encode material stiffness, latent labels or fitted uncertainty. Actual and predicted panels use the same camera and physical scale, with no deformation exaggeration.

Force and torque vectors belong to the right tool, in world coordinates, with torque about its translated tool center. Common material/support scale changes predicted reaction magnitudes but leaves geometry unchanged under this experiment's assumptions.

Case selection is transparent and posthoc: all12 worlds, repeat0, VF physical-risk, lower median improvement and greatest final error in highest-weight normalized physical loss. These are individual physical scenes, not the primary posterior-mean predictions. Numerical observation mismatch remains; the experiment is exploratory.
