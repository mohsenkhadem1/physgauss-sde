   smoothly released support, asymmetric support compliance.

   heights/orientations and repeated contact, with visible deformation.
4. Finite-mass rigid-striker impact: changes in mass, radius, speed and direction.
5. Two deformable objects: collision and stacking with new material pairings.
6. Heterogeneous soft/stiff regions: changed load point and support configuration.
