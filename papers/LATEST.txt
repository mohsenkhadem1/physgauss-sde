Three separate studies: native ReconPhys drop ambiguity, a compliant stem
with proceed/look/push decisions, and nonlinear bowl-contact stress.
The bowl study exposes overconfidence under numerical-model mismatch;
a predeclared mixture repairs the constructed mismatch by including the
finer model. Strong fixed/simple controls remain competitive.
This addendum is not a new end-to-end RGB model or a submitted paper.
