The new study jointly estimates spatial material/support/load fields, integrates
unknown temporal forces, and evaluates camera versus calibrated-probe evidence.
Its inputs are known geometry and tracked surface movement, not raw RGB alone.
The CVPR-format manuscript has eight content pages plus references. It has not
been submitted; the anonymous submission identifier remains unset.

papers/cvpr2027-physical-response/main.pdf. Its unchanged historical supplement
