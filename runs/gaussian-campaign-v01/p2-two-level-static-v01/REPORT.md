# Two-level numerical acceleration and preserved spatial failure

The new additive coarse-P2 preconditioner reproduces the existing 12000-tet transverse equilibrium from rest to **6.5642e-13 m** over every node. It then solves the same 49152-tet physical problem from rest in **49.23 s**, with maximum 124 PCG iterations per Newton step. The full stage took 59.78 s (61.91 s including controller startup). The old 12000 scalar-Jacobi solve took 63.26 s versus 10.33 s here, with maximum CG count falling from 947 to 85. These are recorded run timings, not a controlled hardware benchmark.

The fine original free residual is 9.50e-13 N, force balance defect 2.31e-12 N and actual moment defect 1.76e-14 Nm. The clamp is exact; sampled J>=0.901118 and the sufficient global J lower bound is 0.425229. The numerical acceleration changes no physical stiffness, mass, force, constitutive law or residual threshold. It uses FP64 exact node blocks plus a fixed 4x2x2 coarse rest-tangent correction, literal projected transpose restriction and full consistent coarse mass. Its GPU constant/affine, adjoint and additive-SPD controls passed before any solve.

The spatial criterion still **fails**. All original 63 fixed material probes are retained, including corners. Adjacent maximum displacement differences for nx=16,20,24,32 are 46.2461, 28.3379 and 32.4451 um. Because the last refinement ratio is 32/24, the declared assumed-p>=1 fine indicator is delta/(32/24-1)=**3*delta=97.3354 um**, above 20 um. Unequal refinement ratios mean raw adjacent differences need not decrease. This engineering indicator is not a rigorous continuum bound.

The fine peak principal strain is 13.6944%, exceeding the original 2-8% target; the volume-weighted 99th percentile is 6.1211%, volume RMS strain 1.6212% and volume nonrigid displacement RMS 1.47655 mm. These distinct metrics are reported without discarding clamp-edge samples or using a bulk metric to rescue the peak target. A correct equilibrium is not automatically a spatially resolved training field.

Physics stays E=4500 Pa, nu=0.35, rho=625 kg/m3, the 0.24x0.08x0.08 m block, full left clamp, fixed 0.12 N downward uniform right-face dead traction, gravity zero and volume quadrature3. All rest fields, original control compiler and force/moment definitions remain unchanged. Quadrature4, dynamics, contact, inference and training remain unqualified by this stage.

After the fine original physical gates passed, root authorized resource-only preemption of the older incomplete duplicate block-CG fine solve on GPU2. Its completed 20736 field and partial/error evidence are retained. Both sessions were reaped and GPUs0/2 were released. Peak observed RSS for the new stage was 2,274,959,360 bytes; peak allocated CUDA memory was 1,855,849,472 bytes, below the frozen 12 GiB/8 GiB caps. No wall/disk limit was reached.
