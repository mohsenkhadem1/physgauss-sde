# Prospective independent dataset admission: source and prior audit

This is a new design draft, not a production admission, launch request or change to C18. No simulation, training, architecture change, reserved response, image, checkpoint or ongoing51-cell evidence was accessed. Only source and the four public TRAIN static recipes were inspected. The current119 authored-force engineering declaration deliberately preserves its original peaks and remains immutable.

The production design should sample public hardware first, then hidden physics, and use the same externally randomized known action menu and actuator clock for every hidden condition. A calibrated inference benchmark must generate truth and reference/amortized inference from the same explicitly normalized conditional prior. It should judge future deformation and useful measurements even when several physical explanations remain indistinguishable.

## What the source establishes

1. **The passive peak alone identifies point-pin if it is made public.** `experiments/isaac_dataset.py:95–96` samples `[.12,.24]N` then multiplies by.25 for point-pin. Thus point-pin lies in `[.03,.06]N`, disjoint from other BCs in `[.12,.24]N`; threshold.09N identifies that category without a response. This is not an allegation that the existing passive image encoder actually reads that private peak. It becomes an oracle-correlated known input if recipe forces are exposed as public commands while inference assumes independent BC/action selection. Engineering119 appropriately preserves the recipe and cannot establish production prior factorization. Both existing physical priors also retain this scaling in their *passive* decodes (`campaign_physical_prior.py:320–321`, `campaign_rejection_prior.py:290`). Their explicit known-control override replaces it, which is the appropriate intervention pattern.

2. **Public hardware is chosen after hidden supports.** `isaac_dataset.py:69–89` chooses1/16/9/16 support nodes according to BC, excludes nodes within.027m of that fixture, then selects the two nine-node load patches. Public hardware therefore has a selection relationship with hidden supports. Complete hardware is valid and necessary side information; hiding its patches would reintroduce the diagnosed forward omission. The remedy is a correctly declared `p(BC,support|H)` or a new H-first generator, not pretending this reverse conditional is the original unconditional uniform support generator. A new H-first designed prior may deliberately have uniform BC probabilities given H, but it is a different benchmark distribution.

3. **Do not claim a material-to-amplitude rule that is not present.** Original48 recipes repeat nuisance draws within material triplets (`isaac_dataset.py:26–35`); their Latin-square split restricts load/BC combinations within each split (`:36`). The fresh128 TRAIN construction enumerates all4×4 categories and draws material separately with `seed+100000` (`prepare_gaussian_boundary_study.py:12–24`). Geometry/force code does not inspect E/rho/nu/drag. Deterministic seeds and IDs can identify a finite recipe, but there is no source-backed direct E-dependent force multiplier here.

4. **IDs and source metadata are shortcuts if admitted as features.** In the fresh plan, `index=16*rep+4*li+bi`, so the numeric TRAIN episode suffix modulo4 identifies BC (`prepare_gaussian_boundary_study.py:16–20`). Recipe/source hashes, paths and known recipe seeds can identify the same finite table. Keep these in private audit provenance, never a neural feature. Do not fit a normalization transform using true episode stiffness or future response amplitude. Supervised TRAIN loss weighting may legitimately use TRAIN targets; this does not authorize target-dependent encoder normalization at evaluation.

5. **Camera randomization needs isolation, with a narrower claim.** Camera values are drawn after support/handle selection from the same seeded geometry RNG (`isaac_dataset.py:97–102`). Changing a hidden category need not preserve the same camera realization under a counterfactual replay. This is a reproducibility/identification concern; shared RNG alone does *not* prove population-level BC correlation because the stated camera ranges are common. No metadata classifier or statistical camera-leak accuracy was measured. Use a separately keyed camera stream shared across a fixed-H counterfactual cohort so independence is directly testable. The existing flag `path_depends_on_hidden_motion=False` describes no response-following camera behavior; it does not certify the complete prior factorization.

6. **There is already a good fixed-action pattern.** `isaac_calibrated_probes_v03.py:34–52` gives every case the same.02N x/y/z pulse and z shear menu, plus a.015N diagonal evaluation. It records selection before opening only the selected outcome. Its camera/hardware are known side information, and unknown supports repeat during a fresh rest reset. Keep this information-access pattern, extend it to full hardware and explicitly known command clocks, and qualify the independent mechanics instead of assuming that the old native capture proves force fidelity.

7. **Forecast and gap filling must remain distinct.** The original sparse frame list includes frames40–60 after an internal missing interval (`isaac_dataset.py:17`). Those observations can support gap filling. A future-motion test must use an explicit prefix allowlist and deny all later RGB/depth/masks, even when paths for those frames exist. Planned future commands/camera poses may be public; future measured responses are not.

The exact source hashes are saved in `source-audit.json`. These are statements about the generator and API contracts, not conclusions drawn from reserved outcomes.

## Prior and normalizer contracts

`ConditionalPhysicalPrior` is explicitly a **new designed** hardware-conditional distribution, not the seeded generator's reverse conditional and not a slice of a learned25D density. It samples E log-uniform[1500,20000]Pa, physical-uniform rho[400,850],nu[.28,.42],drag[.35,.85], and a normalized finite support atlas. Within BC it gives feasible faces equal probability, then certified cells equal probability; this is not area-uniform over all feasible faces. Its12D intrinsic density includes log-rho/log-drag Jacobians, categorical masses and proper inactive nuisances (`campaign_physical_prior.py:232–271`). No25D physical-space Lebesgue density is defined.

The full2×9 public attachment IDs must be supplied. Centers alone do not certify support/attachment clearance. The new complete hardware interface additionally carries exact ordered patches, mass/full inertia/COM/orientation and fixed masks. Existing prior `decode` still emits the old25D vector; it is not a valid complete forward adapter. For known controls, passive amplitude/direction/family nuisance entries can remain normalized but inactive; the forward query must use the explicit known program. Unknown passive loads must stay latent, with a prospectively declared BC-independent nuisance prior for the new factorized benchmark. They must not be copied from the recipe into image conditioning.

`ConditionalRejectionPrior` is an alternative **different** distribution, proportional to its kernel divided by unknown `Z_BC(H)` (`campaign_rejection_prior.py:254–271`). It intentionally has no `log_prob`. Within-BC, fixed-H kernel ratios are valid. A genuine whole-prior independence proposal cancels the prior. Using its kernel for category-changing target ratios, BC Bayes factors, flow importance weights or ELBO prior terms is invalid without normalizers. `campaign_tempered_reference.py:96–104,125–127,185–190` enforces normalization strata for local proposals. A capped rejection failure aborts the operation; it cannot redraw/drop a BC, return partial truth or become a null MH move.

For a first new benchmark, use the normalized atlas prior for truth generation, amortization labels and numerical posterior reference. Do not generate atlas truth but evaluate a rejection prior and call that matching-prior inference. Freeze its public-H-conditioned fingerprint and all inactive factors. If a broader rejection prior is later preferred, change generator/reference/learning together under a separate declaration.

Pure source/static tests confirmed that each of the four existing TRAIN public hardware contexts admits every BC in the full-patch certified atlas. Feasible-face counts for point/fixed/two/moving are respectively:

For each H,256 sampled intrinsic states had finite normalized density; overriding the known pulse with[.01,0,0]N produced identical force vectors across BCs. This demonstrates **geometric/prior feasibility only**. It establishes no deformation, material, force-direction or sensor operating envelope. `audit_sources.py` reproduces these checks without opening any response.

## Concrete prospective query/action design

`prospective-config.json` specifies the proposed H-first joint distribution, masks, clock, candidate force tiers and admission gates. H is supplied completely before any BC/material draw. The BC prior is uniform4 given each accepted H; every H must retain support for all four BCs. Material, menu randomization and camera seeds use separate namespaces. H may legitimately change action bases; for fixed H/menu replicate, all menu values, clocks, costs and camera programs must be identical under all hidden material/BC counterfactuals.

Use fixed H2/S2/K1 capacities, two nine-node handles, mass.001kg each and complete measured inertia/COM/pose. Keep inactive support capacity private with a zero mask rather than shortening public tensors. Always include the known command channel, including the all-zero action. Unknown support membership and prescribed sine-squared support law remain latent. No `public_known_support_program` is supplied simply because moving support is known to the simulator. If later a real support actuator is instrumented and known, that is a different observation contract.

The draft proposes a common120Hz actuator clock,3s duration and61 outputs at20Hz. The 12-action primary menu is zero plus three world-axis pulse, three pull-hold, three shear and two equal/opposite couple directions. World-axis signs/permutation use an externally randomized public menu seed. The couple basis uses only public handle separation. Forces act at actual moving handle origins, not a fixed applied torque. All branches have declared cost1 and initially one acquired reset measurement. Identical low menus across all latents prevent truth-conditioned “safe action” choices from announcing the BC.

The proposed primary peak is.01N per handle, with a separately enabled common.02N tier. **Neither tier/menu is qualified for the new joint distribution.** The numbers are engineering candidates, not inferred guarantees: the completed small-force test used one particular[.01,.01,.005]N direction on four specific layouts/materials; the old fixed.02N menu used the unqualified native mechanics; and engineering119 changes quadrature/BC-specific peaks. A smaller resultant or a passing larger-amplitude case does not prove nonlinear behavior for another direction/support/material. Do not enable.12–.24N only for “strong” latent BCs, or retain a hidden point-pin quarter multiplier. Any global tier change requires a prospective TRAIN decision applied to every condition, with old failures retained.

A possible bounded pilot after the current gate is576 counterfactual conditions: four public H×four BC×three shared material anchor tuples×12 common actions, before independent refinement/accuracy/reset controls. The proposed anchors use E1500/5000/20000Pa with rho625kg/m³,nu.35,drag.60s^-1 across every H/BC. These are **new hypothetical material states**, not existing qualified truth. This count is a design option only, not an execution plan or resource authorization. Continuous matching-prior coverage, numerical controls, rendering and split sizes still need their own predeclared bounds and source seals.

## Two-clock gap to resolve before production

The current `HeldCommandQuery` uses one `boundary_times_s` array for known force intervals and latent support endpoints (`campaign_full_hardware_context.py:248–271`). Production cannot silently turn a private, BC-dependent integration grid into a public command grid. Nor may it assume an unspecified between-endpoint support interpolation.

The next version must separate public actuator time from private integration/support evaluation. Compile a known120Hz piecewise-constant force history once; private refinements repeat those exact values instead of reevaluating/requantizing a smooth waveform at each solver rate. Supply the *complete latent analytic support law* and evaluate it explicitly at private solver endpoints. Keep the law truth and step grid outside public image conditioning. A common conservative global solver rate is the simplest first production implementation; its actual value remains unset until qualification with this fixed held command history. The draft's force-only adapter demonstrates exact repeated commands and invariant held-program impulse. It deliberately does not interpolate latent support endpoints or implement the future physical adapter.

This is a required prospective contract change, not an edit to the frozen full-hardware interface or current engineering captures.

## Anti-oracle admission and evaluation

The draft executable accepts only public origins, external menu seed and global tier, rejecting BC/material/support/recipe/solver arguments. Its manifest checker requires the complete BC/material counterfactual crossproduct, fixed capacities and identical menu/clock/camera/schema fingerprints per public context. Twelve offline tests pass in.25s, including command repetition under120/240/480/960/1920Hz private rates, exact zero-resultant couples, translated public geometry, rejection of class-specific metadata/clock changes, missing/duplicate categories and undeclared amplitude tiers. These are implementation-contract tests, not proof that a caller's RNG seed was truth-independent: the complete generated manifest and source dependency graph must also be checked before capture.

A future production audit must preserve failed numerical/sensor slots without latent-specific amplitude clamping, dropping objects or replacement. Such filtering changes `p(theta,A|H)` and generally introduces an unknown outcome-dependent normalizer. Numerical failure is not a legitimate zero observation likelihood. Pause admission and make a new global prospective source/design decision. Geometric atlas validity is a prior definition known before dynamics; physics success is not the same kind of normalized restriction.

Split all sibling latent/material/action/reset branches of a public-H cohort together before dynamics. Cover all families and BCs in each split; never use IDs as model features. Use prefix0,.25,.5,.75,1s for causal forecast and target1.05–3s. Report gap filling separately. A held-out reset-force response is a new experiment from rest, not the same rollout's future state. Unknown supports/motion persist across branches, and replacement of passive loads by known control is explicit. For any future uncontrolled passive task, unknown force amplitude/family/timing stays a marginalized nuisance; a publicly known poke may be added without exposing passive truth.

Before active comparison, freeze same-prior known-parameter forward accuracy and held-out-force predictive coverage/proper scores, calibrated sensor/surrogate discrepancy and a small numerical posterior reference using the identical known query. Do not count the passive video twice when updating an already conditioned posterior. Permit multimodal or broad parameter posteriors; use SBC and predictive coverage/risk by BC/material/action stratum rather than demanding unique material identification. Active and random/fixed baselines receive the same public menu, full hardware, budget and costs, and log selection before observing only the chosen outcome. Candidate fantasies use predictions and calibrated discrepancy, never unselected measurements or true parameters.

No architecture choice is proposed here. Actual-force qualification remains the prerequisite. Production admission, native/continuum fidelity, prediction, physical inference and policy success remain false.
