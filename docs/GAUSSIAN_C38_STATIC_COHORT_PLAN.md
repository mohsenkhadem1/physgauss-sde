# Proposed static RGB-D cohort — no domain admission or draws yet

The completed both-ends gravity check supports one operating point: E=100 kPa, ν=0.35, ρ=625 kg/m³, g=9.81 m/s², the 0.24×0.08×0.08 m prism and fixed end faces. Its finest conditional spatial indicator is 14.24 µm, with about 4.5% peak strain and 0.436 mm bulk nonrigid motion. This is not qualification of a material population, a second support layout, images, or dynamics. The nominal right-face traction in that check acts on fixed nodes; gravity drives its deformation.

The proposed first family retains that geometry, known density/gravity/Poisson ratio and the **polynomial stable-NH** law, with unknown E in 50–150 kPa. This differs from the logarithmic-NH analytical bar. Candidate supports are fixed end faces and the existing two bottom patches `x≤0.06 m or x≥0.18 m`; uniform x divisions must be multiples of four. These are two fixed layouts, not evidence of general unseen-layout inference.

Known additional forces should act on a free material-frame surface patch, rather than the fixed end face. A candidate is a smooth normalized midspan top patch, with prospective signed vertical resultant in [−2,2] N, identical for every material/support hypothesis. Patch normalization, consistent P2 surface quadrature, area and actual force/moment must be recorded. No force is scaled or chosen from a hidden material or support. Lateral loads, changing patch locations and torques require additional qualification rather than implicit admission.

Before drawing objects, freeze the exact patch/law/catalog and qualify both material endpoints × both supports × both force extremes, plus a predeclared interior check set. Retain gravity-only controls. Use the exact-bulk quadrature or a separately proven equivalent. Each domain check needs the original residual, force/moment balance, exact constraints, positive J and whole-P2-element certificate, independent CPU/GPU parity, and a ≤20 µm spatial indicator on common physical probes. Surface/depth tessellation error is a separate gate. Corner and finite interior checks are engineering evidence, not a rigorous uniform error bound. Every later captured state still needs its physical checks; a fixed subset needs independent fine-mesh audits, with no dropping failed cases.

Tentatively assign 128 independent whole objects as 64 TRAIN /16 DEV /16 CAL /32 TEST, balanced by support. Counts, seeds, realized objects and force sequences are **not frozen or generated**. All views, query states and counterfactual actions of an object remain in its role. Exact E values and entire control sequences must be disjoint across roles. Repeated support categories and interpolation within a material range must be named honestly; hash-disjoint tuples alone do not prove unseen-layout or extrapolation performance. A later layout-OOD extension needs additional conforming patch edges and its own qualification matrix.

Use two input tracks. Oracle A receives the true material/support as an explicitly privileged conditional-forward diagnostic. Inference B receives rest geometry and compatible material Gaussians, camera calibration, acquired **past** RGB-D and known acquired/planned forces. It receives no E, support mask/type, full-state positions, candidate truth IDs or future depth. The Gaussian physical shape covariance follows the shared P2 field; posterior uncertainty about material/support remains a separate quantity. Do not add dummy rigid handles or reuse the old two-handle model.

The first study predicts a withheld **static equilibrium under a new known load**, from earlier acquired equilibrium views. It is causal information use, not temporal forecasting. Files should use load-state IDs and acquisition order, not invented velocities, time steps or settling times. Held-camera reconstruction at an observed load and prediction under a withheld load are separate scores. Dense RGB-D is also distinct from a 72-ray idealized depth likelihood or privileged tracked points. RGB appearance/camera randomization must be independent of material/support labels; no truth-colored overlays enter model images.

Reuse `campaign_c38_data.py` admission/path binding, the P2 Gaussian transport and curved-surface sampler, and the existing censored range model. Recheck depth/visibility refinement on actual admitted shapes, including disagreeing/missing rays. Do not call sparse ranges dense RGB-D. No foundation model or new learned architecture is justified before data and baselines reveal a need. A conditional static forward map has no physical history dependence; prefix history belongs to inference about unknown parameters, not a fictitious static GRU time evolution.

Baseline comparisons should include rest/persistence, past-only rigid registration, a candidate-conditioned linear FEM response with the same body/surface loads and constraints, a proper material/support prior predictive, and a privileged true-parameter nonlinear FEM oracle. B baselines have the same acquired observations and unknown-parameter budget; the oracle is labeled separately. Full-state baselines cannot silently replace RGB-D baselines. Diagnose oracle-forward error before blaming inference. Reuse a small converged material-plus-discrete-support reference before making uncertainty claims; the failed neural bar posteriors or partially qualified importance clouds do not satisfy that gate.

Only after DEV/CAL numerical and uncertainty checks pass should TEST be released. Report object-level errors, physical deformation and image/depth scores, interval widths, coverage with binomial uncertainty, joint/reference gaps and paired object bootstrap intervals. Any active comparison uses an independent reset probe, equal acquired-frame/noise/force budgets and the **same** canonical target for every policy. Freeze choices before outcomes, and predictions before target labels; compare fixed, random and an obvious geometric heuristic as well as model risk. Acquisition duration is an accounting assumption until dynamics demonstrate settling. Broad dynamic forecasting/contact remains a separate unqualified track.
