# C38 probing choices: the fixed-down limitation

All 64 gravity-only observations now have saved nonlinear-table and linear-model
predictions. All 128 executions pass the independent posterior integration
checks. Two controller failures caused by reading an optional process-memory
status field during worker exit are preserved. The corrected controller reads
the kernel resident-page measurement; complete model outputs were reused by
hash, and interrupted calculations were explicitly continued with the same
observations. No sensor noise or object identities were regenerated.

Both information gain and canonical-target variance reduction select the
downward 2 N probe for every record, under both models. All numerical cloud
checks pass; no numerical or Monte Carlo fallback was needed. The random
baseline chooses down 34 times and up 30 times. These choices were saved before
any actual probe measurement was released.

This means the two active criteria are exactly the fixed-down policy in this
declared action space. They cannot demonstrate an advantage over fixed-down,
regardless of the later outcome scores. Whether the extra measurement improves
prediction and whether intervals remain reliable are separate pending questions.
Both fixed-action counterfactuals are being computed from the same once-generated
measurements; each policy receives one 27-coordinate acquisition. All 256
post-probe predictions must be saved before the evaluator reads canonical
targets or material/support labels.

A distinct softer-floor diagnostic retained the same block/material, time
interval, mechanics checks and error gates, while changing floor compliance
from 1e9 to 1e6 N/m³. It passes its mechanics checks but still fails spatial
convergence: finest conditional indicators are 456.36 µm and 0.39934 m/s, versus
20 µm and 0.0001 m/s. The residual difference is predominantly elastic motion.
The intended compliant floor permits about 7 mm penetration; it is not a hard
floor model. Neither this diagnostic nor the original drop is admitted as
prediction ground truth.

C39 source prepares a separate action-design screen: middle downward, left
downward and middle lateral loads, each of magnitude 2 N. The compiler accepts
only geometry and the public action; material and support labels are not inputs.
The left patch uses exact quadratic-triangle shape integrals and retains the
correct force moment. This does not alter the frozen C38 comparison.

The screen now confirms a difference on graded32/40 meshes: end clamps favour
the downward probe by a factor of 1.196 in local stiffness Fisher information;
bottom supports favour the lateral probe by a factor of 2.809. All twelve
rest-tangent response solves pass original-operator residual checks, and the
largest conditional point-displacement indicators are 6.10 and 15.58 µm. This is
a public-model design diagnostic, not measured active-policy performance.
Nonlinear qualification is still required for the lateral action.

Calibration and accuracy results remain pending. Raw data and media stay local;
source, protocols, compact controls and negative results are published.
