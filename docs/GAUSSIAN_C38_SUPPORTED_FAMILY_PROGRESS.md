# Supported blocks: deformation is informative, spatial accuracy still incomplete

The 14 declared material/support/force cases have finished. All 42 nonlinear mesh solves and all 14 independent coarse CPU/GPU checks pass their physical gates. **Seven of the 14 cases pass the 20 µm displacement-refinement indicator; seven fail. No varied dataset is admitted.** The [complete matrix](../runs/operator-dev/c38-static-prequalification-v01/REPORT.md) includes every case. Exit zero records completion of the bounded experiment, not scientific success.

The block has known density and gravity, Young modulus 75–150 kPa, and either both fixed end faces or two fixed bottom patches. The same public top-midspan forces −2, 0 and +2 N act independently of material and support. The constitutive law is polynomial stable Neo-Hookean; this is a stipulated synthetic mechanical model. Every resolution starts from rest and retains the original residual, exact constraints, positive Jacobian, body/surface load and actual wrench checks. The conditional spatial indicator assumes convergence order at least one and uses actual maximum element edges; it is not a rigorous continuum error bound.

The new states contain substantial deformation. A separate read-only diagnostic removes the best volume-weighted rigid translation and rotation at each finest uniform field. Remaining coordinate motion RMS is **46.1–60.3%** of the original motion RMS across these 14 fixed corners/interiors. Positive degree-six volume quadrature is used, and the independent rigid-removal calculation reproduces the original bulk diagnostics within 6.4e-18 m. This is an RMS ratio, not a fraction of kinetic energy. These are selected development cases, not a random population, and this does not establish parameter identifiability or sensor calibration. The [diagnostic](../runs/operator-dev/c38-static-motion-diagnostic-v02/summary.json) binds each input field by hash.

The worst uniform case is the softer material with bottom patches and downward force: its indicator is 73.838 µm. A fixed boundary-focused mesh was authored independently of material/support/load, with nodes concentrated at the same physical patch edges and object surfaces. True T10 physical edge midpoints and affine reference elements were rebuilt after mapping. The proposed mesh has seven CPU geometry/mass/prolongation controls; the force compiler and physical model remain unchanged.

The two-case graded pilot completes all six mesh solves and both independent coarse comparisons with physical checks passing. Its worst-bottom indicator decreases to **33.348 µm**, and the both-end control gives **23.997 µm**. Both still fail 20 µm. Uniform and graded comparisons remain separate refinement sequences, and all 63 original query points are retained. Further graded 32/40 refinement is a new prospective phase; these two pilot cases do not qualify the other 12 cases.

Grading exposes a further limitation: the finest sampled peak strain rises from 9.71% to 31.57% in the bottom case and from 7.66% to 12.08% in the both-end control. Sharp fixed-patch edges can produce unconverged local stress/strain peaks. Bulk nonrigid RMS remains 0.332 and 0.781 mm respectively. A later displacement-indicator pass must not be reported as converged peak stress, uniformly moderate strain, fixture realism or a validated real material law. The [pilot report](../runs/operator-dev/c38-static-graded-pilot-v01/REPORT.md) retains the negative outcomes.
